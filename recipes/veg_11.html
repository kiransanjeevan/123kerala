Simple Mushroom Curry
Ingredients
 1.Mushrooms - 500 gms
2.Pearl Onions(small onions) - 12-15 nos
3.Red Chilly(dry) - 6nos
4.Oil for frying
5.salt to taste

How to Cook:
Make a fine paste of pearl onions and dry red chilly. then mix the paste well with mushrooms and keep it aside for half an hour. heat oil in a pan and add chopped onions(small onions) and fry till it gets brown in colour.Add mushrooms amd cook well when it is done.Also check salt for taste
