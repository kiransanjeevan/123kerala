Forget family planning...have lots of kids: Suresh Gopi

KOCHI: "Children are the best asset for every parent and the society. So, forget family planning, nuclear family and have lots of kids," says actor Suresh Gopi. He was inaugurating the public interaction programme organised as part of the International Congress on Infertility and IUI Workshop, which concluded in Kochi on Sunday.

"Many couples postpone pregnancy, citing financial security and enjoyment of life as the reasons. But they never realise the frustration of not having children even after years of marriage." "It is better to have children as early as possible. Being late means complications," he said.

Dr C Mohammed Ashraf, medical director of the Centre for Research In Assisted Reproduction and Foetal Therapy (CRAFT), Kodungallur, spoke on the importance of least invasive and cost effective treatment for infertility. "Awareness of the problem is more important as it saves the mental, physical and financial strain of the infertility treatment," he said. And couples should not forget the problems associated with delayed pregnancy, as the age of women plays a very important role in conception. For men, the reasons for infertility could be habits like smoking and alcoholism, he said.

Dr R Rajan, president of Indian Academy of Human Reproduction (IAHR) also spoke about the main reasons for infertility and the effectiveness of treatment. New technologies are a solace to couples with male or female reproductive disorders, he said. "Endoscopic surgery has improved the scope of fertility and has escalated the carry-home baby rate." Delegates from all over the country and outside participated in the two-day congress, organised by AIHR and CRAFT, first of its kind in Kochi.

Papers on topics such as the new trends in infertility treatment, new medicines and tubal infertility were presented in today's session. Symposium on Intra Uterine Insemination (IUI) and IUI Workshop was also held.
