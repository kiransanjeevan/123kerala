Stoppage of work on library stayed

KOCHI: Kerala high court on Tuesday stayed the government order directing stoppage of construction work on the state library council complex at Thiruvananthapuram.

The stay was issued by justice K A Abdul Gaffoor while considering a petition filed by I V Das, secretary of State Library Council, challenging a government order dated March 14 this year.

The previous government had allotted 20 cents of government land adjacent to the public library for construction of a building complex of the council. The petitioner claimed that Rs 6 lakh had already been spent for the construction work so far.
