Malayalee doc arrested for selling porno through websites

Chennai, Dec 29, An Orthopaedic surgeon has been arrested on the charge of selling pornographic materials to several countries through various websites in the past six years earning hundreds of thousands of dollars in the process, according to the police.

On a complaint from a student, who himself was a victim to the escapades of the medico, the 43-year-old, Prakash, hailing from Palakkad in Kerala, but settled down in Chennai and running XB for over a decade now, was arrested four days ago. More than 50,000 CDs, digital and video cameras, were seized during raids on his residence in Anna Nagar and farm house in Valasaravakkam.

He was later produced before a city court which remanded him to five days' police custody from Wednesday last. A case had been registered against him under various sections of Information Technology Act, Indecent Representation of Women Act, besides various sections of IPC and Arms Act.

Police said the doctor lured several college girls and working women by offering them large sums of money to shoot the porno videos and nude pictures. With the help of his US-settled brother, who was adept in computer graphics, the medico sold the porno materials through the website, created by him with his brother's help, and through other websites, police said.

Police said the medico, who was eager to make fast bucks, was advised by his brother that the porno market, through website, was huge. The doctor then started using even his para-medical staff to shoot the videos.

The doctor's escapades came to notice of the police when a student, hailing from Pondicherry, lodged a complaint, stating that the doctor had uploaded his pictures on the website, by means of trick photography. He said he had refused to pose for the websites, though the doctor had asked him to do so at pistol point.
