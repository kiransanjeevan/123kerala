HC staff to abstain from strike

Kochi: A division Bench of the Kerala High Court today asked government to take all steps to ensure that public life was not affected by the indefinite strike called by the state government employees from tomorrow, even as the High Court staff decided not to participate in the strike.

The observation was made by the Bench, comprising justice G Sivarajan and justice M Ramachandran, on a public interest litigation by M L George, president Focus-N, a social organisation, seeking prohibition of the strike using the penal provisions of the ESMA. While the matter came up for consideration, the government pleader submitted that all steps had been taken by the government to effectively cope with the situation.

While closing the petition, the Bench held that being a policy matter it was for the government to take appropriate measures to see that public life was not affected by the strike. “We are sure the government shall take all steps in this regard”, the bench said.

Meanwhile, two staff associations of the High Court challenged the order of Chief Justice B N Srikrishna making the ESMA applicable to the High Court service. The matter was heard in part by Justice K A Abdul Gaffoor and was deferred for further hearing. In the light of the pendency of the original petition, the staff associations decided not to participate in the strike.
