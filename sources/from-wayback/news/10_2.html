'Theerthadanam' best Malayalam Film

Chennai: 'Theerthadanam' has been adjudged the best Malayalam film for 2001 and would receive the 'Mathrubhumi-Medimix Film Award, announced here. The film will receive a cash prize of Rs 1.50 lakh. Jayaram has been chosen best actor and Samyuktha Varma, best actress, for their roles in 'Theerthadanam' and 'Megha Malhar' respectively, each of whom will receive a cash award of Rs 50,000 at a function to be held in Kochi on March 31, chairman of the jury, Balu Mahendra, told a news conference here.

Nedumudi Venu and Purnima Mohan have been adjudged best character actor and best character actress for their roles in 'Ishtam' and 'Megha Malhar'. Each of them will receive a cash award of Rs 20,000. In all, 10 films were seen by the jury members, Mahendra, who started his career in Malayalam films as a cinematographer three decades ago, said. Over 90 films were produced in Kerala last year. Life Time Achievement Award will also be given on March 31, and the name of the recipient is yet to be finalised.

Mahendra regretted that the quality of Malayalam films produced during last year had "deteriorated considerably", compared to those produced some years ago. "Theme, form and content" had gone down now, he said.
