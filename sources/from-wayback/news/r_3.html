HC sets aside Pan Masala ban

KOCHI: The ban imposed by the Director of Health Services on the manufacture, storage, distribution and sale of pan masala was set aside by a Division Bench of the Kerala High Court today.

Pan masala containing tobacco or any other ingredient injurious to health, under whatever name or description it is sold in the State of Kerala, had been banned for one year from July 1, 2001 in the interest of public health. N Naushad of Alappuzha and several others tobacco merchants challenged the ban order.

The impugned order had been issued following a direction from the High Court on a public interest writ petition filed by Lahari Virudha Samithi, Thalikkulam, seeking to ban the sale and distribution of pan masala. The court had taken the view that it was open to the State Government to issue a suitable notification.

Under the provisions of Prevention of Food Adulteration Act the Director is authorised to control and supervise the duties and operation of the food inspectors and local authorities under the Act and rules thereunder.

In States like Gujarat and Karnataka PFA Rules confer specific powers on the Health Director to ban pan masala. In the absence of any rule to his effect, the director has no jurisdiction to issue a total bank on pan masala containing the tobacco, the Bench consisting of Justice K S Radhakrishnan and Justice K Radhakrishnan Nair said.
