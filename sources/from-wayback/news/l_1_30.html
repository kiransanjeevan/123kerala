Major cut in STD rates

 

IN ANOTHER bonanza for telecom users, the government on Friday announced slashing of long-distance rates by more than 60 per cent, a move that will result in Bharat Sanchar Nigam sacrificing about Rs 3,000 crore in six months.

Announcing the new rates, communication minister Pramod Mahajan said the revised tariffs would be effective from January 14 next year.

There would be only two time slabs: the peak period -- from 9 am to 8 pm -- and offpeak period -- from 8 pm to 9 am.

Also there would be only three distance slabs -- between 50 and 200 km; 200 to 500 km; and 500 km and above. For the first distance slab of 50-200 km, the STD rates have been halved from Rs 4.80 per minute to Rs 2.40 during the peak hours, while for the 200-500 km slab, the rates has been slashed to Rs 4.80 a minute from existing rate of Rs 11.60 per minute.

For the third distance slab of 500 km and above, rate cut is even sharper falling to Rs 9 a minute from the existing Rs 24 per minute (for 1,000 km and above) and from Rs 17.56 (for 500-1,000 km).

While for the offpeak rates, where three slabs have been merged to one from 8 pm to 9 am, the tariffs for 50-200 km slab remained unchanged at Rs 1.20 per minute, while for the second slab of 200-500 km the tariffs has been reduced to Rs 2.40 a minute from Rs 3 now.

For the distance slab of 500 km and above, the tariffs during offpeak hour would be Rs 4.50 as against the prevailing Rs 4.50 (500-1,000 km) and Rs 6 per minute (1,000 km and above).

Mahajan said BSNL would suffer a loss of about Rs 3,000 crore during the first six months of revised tariffs, but pointed out that the corporation would emerge as net revenue earner with substantial increase ranging between 50 and 70 per cent in traffic.
