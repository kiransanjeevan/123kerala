US team in Thrissur to record Pooram percussion

THRISSUR: SLKK-LLC, an organisation based in the US, was here on Thursday to document the famous drum ensemble like Pandi Melam and Panchavadyam.

The organisers said that it was part of their effort to record the drum concerts prevalent and popular in every part of the world and study musical rhythm, sound and aspects of fusion.

For that purpose they had visited African and South American countries and have many more places in their itinerary. Though the producers are from the US, the director of the project is from Britain and they have titled the project as `Percussion of the world'.

Peruvanam Kuttan Marar led a team of 50 artistes for Pandi Melam while Sreedharan, son of the late Pallavoor Maniyan Mararm, presented Panchavadyam with a team of 60 artistes. Having witnessed the famous Thrissur Pooram personally and on video, the team chose Thrissur as the ideal place and to capture the ambience of a temple festival, 15 elephants with accompaniments were part of the show.

However, the artistes were disappointed since they were not allowed to perform in the way they were accustomed to. The organisers made them present the art piece-meal so that only selected portions could be assimilated in the final version. Some of the experienced artistes lamented: ``Had we known about the intention of the organisers, we would have refused to take part in the programme. The whole thing was a farce. They were denigrating our melam culture''.

The organisers of the big budget extravaganza, supported by state-of-the-art equipment, made the artistes and tuskers toil right from the morning till about 5 pm under the hot sun. Apparently the enticement was the promise of good remuneration.
