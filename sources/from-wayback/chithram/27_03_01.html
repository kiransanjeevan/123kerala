Bhavana to become a violinist

 

Actress Bhavana is currently not accepting any new projects in any language. Staying in her Trissur house, the actress is even not picking up her phone and she has instructed her assistant to reply all the calls. Don't come to any decision that she will get in to wedlock like her friends Gopika and Karthika or something hasty. As she has a great love for Music, she is seriously learning to play violin (She is also going to appear as a violinist in a forthcoming Tamil film). But Bhavana's passion towards the art, forced her to dedicate her valuable month long schedule to learn the string instrument like a professional. Her instructor Master Gopi is happy with her fast improvement and dedication in learning.

 
 





 
 

The Chithiram Pesuthadi girl is now become hot in Telugu after the hit of her recently released Ontari with Gopichand and she is selectively accepting offers there. She is playing lead role in Jayam Kondan at present and talks going on in two other new films in Tamil.
