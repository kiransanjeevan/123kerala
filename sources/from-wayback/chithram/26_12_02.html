Top Malayalam actors, 2008

 

There's no doubt that the two big Ms of the Malayalam film industry -- Mammootty and Mohanlal -- still call the shots. But 2008 witnessed the emergence of new actors, who made quite an impact on screen. We take a look at the top Malayalam actors of 2008:

Ranjith

Indeed an unlikely choice as an actor, the writer-director unleashed magic on screen as an ageing teacher who still has the fire burning within him in Jayaraj's Gulmohar. His character, Induchoodan, was a firebrand revolutionary in his youth, and his pain towards the woes of suffering people never died down.

It was a role originally meant for Suresh Gopi. But after Gulmohar, viewers could only imagine Ranjith as Induchoodan. Ranjith also directed Thirakkadha this year, and turned lyricist with Pakal Nakshatrangal.

Govind Padmasurya

In M G Sasi's award-winning film, Adayalangal, Govind played Gopi. Govind was still in college, with no acting experience, when he acted in this film. His character lived in anguish, out of poverty and responsibilities. Govind played the part to perfection and came up with a confident performance. He plans to do more films in future, and may be one of the most promising stars to watch out for.

Jayaram

With no hits in recent years, Jayaram had hit real rock bottom in his career. Then Veruthe Oru Bharya happened, and his fortunes changed again. Ironically, Jayaram claimed that he had discouraged director Akku Akbar from casting him in the lead. But his character Sugunan -- a peculiar, chauvinistic middleclass husband -- went down very well with the audiences.

Veruthe Oru Bharya is still running to packed houses today. Jayaram was also noted for his Tamil films this year --

 
 





 
 
Prithviraj

After films like Mozhi and the ongoing Mani Ratnam film, Prithviraj is on a roll. Add two well-written roles in Malayalam to this -- a Naxalite leader Joseph in Madhupal's Thalappavu and film director Akbar Ahmed who delves deep into the life of yesteryear sensation Malavika in Ranjith's Thirakkadha. Both films released on the same day, but Prithviraj added freshness to both characters. He has been long acknowledged as the Next Big Thing in Malayalam cinema, but he has rarely been offered truly challenging roles. This year seems to have changed that.

Mohanlal

The versatile genius had an eventful year, balancing serious and commercial films well. His role as noted film director Sidharthan in Rajeevnath's Pakal Nakshatrangal and as a selfish architect called Albert Samson in K P Kumaran's Akashagopuram gave an altogether different meaning to subtle acting. He had success at the box office as well, with films like Madambi, Kurukshetra, Innathe Chinthavishayam and Twenty 20. His guest role in Mizhikal Sakshi was very good too. With plenty of high profile films in his kitty, Mohanlal is sure to maintain his superstardom in future.
