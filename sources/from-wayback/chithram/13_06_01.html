'No 1 in one year' Mamta’s goal

Mamta Mohandas had learnt her acting from the man who had shaped superstars like Mammooty and Mohanlal, director Hariharan. With that training, she is now ready to shift her attention to Tamil.

“I’m very well aware that it is impossible to survive in the Tamil film industry with glamour alone. That’s why I’m trying to strike a perfect balance between glamour and performance,” Mamta Mohandas said, about her preparations for entering Kollywood.

‘Sivappadhikaram’ is the name of the film and she is going to be Vishal’s heroine.

“My plans are simple. I want to become the No 1 actress in Tamil within a year. I’m not going to accept eachadn every offer that comes my way. I’m going to only accept those films that bring out my acting capabilities. I want people to know me as an actress of caliber,” she said.
