Sathyan's new family entertainer

 

The veteran director Sathyan Anthikad, who has been in the industry for over 30 years, still rules the box-office.

The proof of that is that his new untitled Jayaram film, produced by Kalasangam Hamsa, has been able to get advance from theatres to the tune of Rs 1.60 crore, at a time of economic recession.

Theatres across Kerala prefer Sathyan’s film over Mohanlal’s or Mammootty’s summer release! Actually the shoot of the film is going on in Kuttanad area. The film has Jayaram, Kaniha, Naren, Nedumudi Venu, Innocent, Mamookoya, and K.P.A.C Lalitha.

Jayaram plays a cable operator Benny Chacko in the film, who is always, broke. He has two younger sisters, and they are not able to get married as Benny has no dowry to give.

 
 





 
 
So Benny marries a state agriculture officer's daughter Daisy (Kaniha) hoping to get a fat dowry, so that he can get his sisters settled. But Daisy’s father cons him at the last moment, leading to the twists in the story.

Says Sathyan: “ It is a sentimental family movie laced with humour that will make you laugh at the same time you will get a lump in your throat. There are three melodious songs tuned by Ilayaraja. On the whole it is a feel good family entertainer.”

Sathyan who is working for the first time with cameraman Venu, will complete the shoot by March 25 in and around Kuttanad. He plans to release this untitled film by May 1.
