Three films for X'mas

 

Three big films are due for Christmas this year- Crazy Rascals, Lolipop, Red Chillies. The Sabarimala season has started, so there are unlikely to be any big Malayalam films till Christmas.

Atleast half a dozen films like Suresh Gopi's Bullet are ready for release, but there are hardly any takers. Meanwhile Twenty: 20 is doing extraordinary business, and other films just does not want to clash with it.

 
 





 
 
Lolipop- Release date, December 19
Cast- Prithviraj, Bobban Kunchacko, Jayasurya, Bhavana, Roma
Director- Shafi
Distributor Mulagupadam Films


Crazy Rascals - Release Date, December 20
Cast – Dileep, Salim Kumar, Jagathy, Radha Varma
Director- Deepu Karunakaran
Distributor- Ullatil Films

Red Chillies Release Date, December 25
Cast- Mohanlal and 9 models
Director- Shaji Kailas
Distributors- Vysakh Films
