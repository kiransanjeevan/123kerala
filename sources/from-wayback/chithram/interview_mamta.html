‘Music is my life’: Mamtha

Film industry is a cruel place and only the fittest survives in it. There is no place for unsuccessful actors but Mamtha is an exception. Her films may go down the drains but she still remains the apple to the eyes of the filmmakers and the media alike. She is a trained singer in Carnatic and Hindustani music and even did some modeling assignments before Hariharan spotted her. Now, she has quite a few films lined up to do. Read on to know more about her.

Excerpts from the Interview:

Could you tell us something about yourself and your family?

At present, I am doing B.Sc. final year in Mount Carmel College, Bangalore. I am looking forward to do P.G in U.K. I am the only child in the family. My parents are in Baharin, as my father is the manager of a Bank there. I was brought up there too.

How did you bag the role in ‘Mayookam’?

I never thought of becoming an actress. In fact, I was keen on becoming a singer. As fate has it, Sridevi Aunty introduced me to Hariharan at a party. He then offered me a role in his film. I was reluctant at first but my parents encouraged me to take the plunge. It was not very difficult to face the camera, as I had some modeling experience. I did what Hariharan asked me to do. I am happy for the accolades I received but the credit should go to Hariharan.

Why did you take up the small role in ‘Bus Conductor’?

I was busy with my modeling assignments after ‘Mayookam’ was delayed. At that very juncture, V.M Vinu called me and asked me if I were interested in playing the role of Mamootty’s sister. I gave him the nod. There were butterflies flying in my stomach when I first acted with Mammootty but he along with Vinu helped me out. My role in the film is small but neat.

Tell us something about your film ‘Adbhudam’.

The film was shot within 2 hours and 45 minutes, which is a record. It is all about mercy killing and I played the role of Maria. We took five days’ rehearsal before the actual shooting took place at Ramoji film city in Hyderabad. It also has American and Russian actors besides Malayalam actors. It was a great experience all together and I am proud to be a part of it.

It is said that you have some steamy scenes in your next film ‘Lanka’. Is that right?

My character in the film is very challenging, as I have to portray varied emotions. In, fact, Kareena Kappor was first considered for the role. It deals with deep love and terrorism and I speak lots of Tamil in it. Director A.K Sajan convinced my parents and me regarding certain scenes in the film.

What role do you play in ‘Madhuchandralekha’?

I play the role of a modern girl in this hilarious film. It is a character that I have not played earlier.

What are your forthcoming films?

I have a film titled ‘Keerthi Chakra’ with Mohanlal and Major Ravi is the director. It will be shot at Kargil, as it is about a soldier.

Are you choosy while selecting films?

I take lots of care while selecting films. In fact, I used to take the final decision with my parents. It is quality that matters to me and not quantity. Otherwise, I would rather continue my modeling assignments.

What happened to your singing career?

Music is my life. I am trained in both Carnatic and Hindustani music and I am currently training under Sudaker Devele. I have also sung Bhajans at various events. But I know that my voice is hoarse when it comes to dubbing. I am giving my best to improve voice modulation and make it soft and filmy.
