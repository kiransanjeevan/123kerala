Quick Chat with Jayaran

There was a time when Jayaram-starrers like 'Thoovalkottaaram' outshone even superstar films at the box office. Jayaram continued unabated as the only minimum-guarantee star in Malayalam. He also entered Tamil filmdom with 'Gokulam' and then did films like 'Purushalakshanam', 'Nila' and 'Kolangal'. And with 'Tenali', Jayaram became a sort of rage in Tamil too. But then something happened and his films started growing less frequent. Some films even bombed at the box office. But, his serious roles in 'Shesham' and 'Theerthadanam' won him appreciation as an actor with real acting potential. However, in commercial cinema, it has been a rather lean phase for him over the last couple of years. But, Jayaram is back now, in full swing. His Tamil film 'Naina' is doing well at the box office. He has completed a film by noted director Balu Mahendra too. In Malayalam, he has already completed Satyan Anthikad's film. He is at present doing T.K.Rajeev Kumar's 'Seethakalyanam'. He also has his hands full of very interesting films. Here's Jayaram, in a quick chat ...

Q: Don't you agree that the past few years were rather lack-lustre as far as your career in mainstream cinema was concerned?

A: Yes, I do agree. But the truth is that it was something that I had opted for, to a certain extent at least. Offers did come my way. But in the present context, when Malayalam cinema itself is in a 'not-so-good' condition, I decided not to accept all those films, which were mostly trash anyway.

Q: Things seem to be going smooth again. Is it?

A: Malayalam cinema seems to have picked up momentum again in the recent months. And consequently I am getting good films again. I have just finished Satyan Anthikad's film. And now I am doing T.K.Rajeev Kumar's film. Then, there are films by Sibi Malayil, Lohitadas, Kamal etc. All these directors, as you know, are well-known directors, and so getting offers from them should surely be a good sign.

Q: Please tell us more about these new films of yours?

A: In Satyan Anthikad's film, which has been named 'Yatrakkarude Shradhayku', I am playing a character named Ramanujan, an engineer working in a construction company in Chennai. It is a very good character and the film, in which I have starred opposite Soundarya, will be a real treat for film-lovers. In Rajeev Kumar's 'Seethakalyanam', in which I am acting opposite Jyothika, I am playing a young Brahmin software engineer, who insists on the traditional seven-day long Brahmin marriage. A very lively character indeed, and a different and interesting film too. In Lohitadas' forthcoming film, I play a Namboothiri youth sharing a strong attachment with his elephant. In Sibi Malayil's film, I am acting along with my son, Kalidas, with whom I had earlier acted in Satyan Anthikad's 'Kochu Kochu Santhoshangal'.

Q: Could you tell us about your experience doing 'Shesham'?

A: It was a real good experience, unforgettable in every way. Portraying the character of Lonappan in the film taught me much about acting and cinema. It has helped the actor in me become more mature. The character fetched me the Critics' Award and the prestigious V.Shantaram Award too.

Q: What about 'Theerthadanam'?

A: As you know very well, the character that I played in the film was one that was created by noted writer M.T.Vasudevan Nair. And therefore 'Theerthadanam' meant much to me. The character was very sensitive in nature and I liked playing it.

Q: Is it the so-called art films or the so-called commercial films that give you more satisfaction?

A: I don't like to classify films as art and commercial. Still, since the classification almost exists in the minds of people, let me make my stand clear. I am much indebted to commercial cinema, because without them, I wouldn't have been what I am now. And if I speak from an artist's perspective, any character that he plays, be it in an art film or a commercial one, gives him satisfaction.

Q: How are things going in Tamil?

A: 'Naina' is doing well at the theatres. I have earned myself a place in the hearts of Tamil film-lovers too. And have got some really exciting and big projects in Tamil too, which will commence soon.
