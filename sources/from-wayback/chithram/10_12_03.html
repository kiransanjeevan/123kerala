Suresh Gopi and Kunchacko Boban in Rajasenan's new film

Suresh Gopi and teenage sensation Kunchacko Boban are to come together in Rajasenan's new film, 'Swapnamkondu Thulaabhaaram'. The film will be produced under the banner of Hash Bush Films by Khader Hassan, who had produced Rajasenan's last film 'Nakshatrakannulla Rajakumaran Avanundoru Rajakumari'. Also in the cast are Jagathy Sreekumar, Sreevidya, Janardhanan etc. The film is based on a script by Reghunath Paleri and will have lyrics written by S.Rameshan Nair and Gireesh Puthencherry. Music is by Ouseppachchan and cinematography by Ramachandra Babu. Shooting is to commence by mid-December at Thodupuzha.
