'Kuruvi' makes a big release

 

'Kuruvi'- the new film from ace actor Vijay made a big release in Kerala. The movie got released in more than 55 theatres across the state. In all the important metros, the movie is being released in at least three theatres. Directed by hit maker Dharani, the movie features Trisha, Suman and Manivannan with Vijay.

This is Dharani's fifth film in Tamil after a memorable hat trick of super hits in ‘Dhil’, ‘Dhool’ and ‘Gilli’. Every one in the trade is in the lookout whether he can make it four in a row. ‘Kuruvi’ is also the fourth film of the celebrated Vijay-Trisha pair. Produced by Udayanidhi Stalin, the movie features six songs set to music by Vidyasagar.

 
 





 
 

Malaysian rapper Yogi B and Dr. Burn also croon for the film. The movie as usual, is an all-out action adventure laced with romance and family sentiments. Vijay who is much satisfied with the way the film has shaped up, expects 'Kuruvi' to do better than ‘Gilli’, his blockbuster of 2004 from the same director.
