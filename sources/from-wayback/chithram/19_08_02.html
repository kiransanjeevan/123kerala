Rambha promises a big comeback

 

A casual mention of the name Mango Dolly is more than enough to get actress Rambha And once that happens, the actress’s eyes are gleaming and how! “Mango Dolly will be going to Mumbai soon to promote the film,” she chirps excitedly.

It might seem a bit odd, but the actress refers to her character in her upcoming Bollywood film — Quick Gun Murugun —like an outsider. “That’s because everybody called me by that name on the sets,” she starts off, “No one called me Rambha! So much so, that I’ve almost forgotten my own name now.”

Call it a role with a difference, and that’s exactly what she thinks too. “It seriously is,” she states, thumping the table softly in emphasis, “Would you believe me if I said that I didn’t understand the story the first time I heard it?”

Now, that’s some serious revelation and she’s only too eager to divulge details. “I asked the director (Shashank Ghosh) to give me the full script,” she recalls, “And very soon, he was there with the whole Quick Gun... team. We didn’t have enough chairs at home to make them comfortable.”

The director, full of creative ideas and wacky themes, was so excited seeing his ‘Mango Dolly’ in person, that he goofed up a bit. “I couldn’t understand anything when he narrated the story,” she says, “It was all too quick to digest.”

However, a few months later, the director promptly landed up at Rambha’s residence again and this time, he was better prepared. “He gave me the dialogues too and it had me in splits,” she says.

 
 





 
 
That was the case on the sets too, where she got to meet Rajendra Prasad and the rest of the cast. “I’ve never been spoon-fed like this before,” she says, “I was assigned a man Friday, who took care of all my needs on the sets. Quick Gun... is a kind of film that every actress ought to do at least once in her career,” she says. One of the many thrills that she got while shooting was wearing the blonde wig. “I’ve already worn a blonde wig for a fleeting shot in Kadhalar Dhinam and I knew that I’d look good in it,” she says, “But the team didn’t know that! They had arranged almost 20 different types of wigs for me to try on and choose from. I had one look at all of them and chose one. And guess what, it fit me perfectly!”

Rambha has two characters in the film that are like chalk and cheese and the variety they offered was a major attraction for her to choose the role. “One of them is supposed to be sexy and the other one is a normal person,” she says, not willing to disclose any more details.

So, what’s the plan post Quick Gun? “I really don’t know,” she says, “Every time I think that my career is gone, something interesting comes up. I guess I’m like a cat with nine lives!”
