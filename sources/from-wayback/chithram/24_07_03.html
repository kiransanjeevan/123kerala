Gajala - Another actress who want to die!

Just as Kollywood was recovering from the suicide of actresses Pratyuksha and Monal, another upcoming star has downed the pills. Actress Gajala, who recently starred opposite Arjun in Ezhumalai which is still in the theatres, took an overdose of sleeping pills on Sunday. The actress was found unconscious in her hotel room (she had gone to Hyderabad for a shoot) on early Monday morning. Actor Arjun who was also in the city shooting for Sarvadhikari (Gajala's his co-star in it) rushed to her hotel room and admitted the actress in a hospital. After intensive treatment, the actress was declared 'safe' later. Buzz has it that the actress, who was in love with her Kalusukovalani co-star Uday Kiron had begun to suspect that he was two-timing her, because he had recently begun to avoid her. It is said that she had then pleaded with him to marry her. But, the upcoming Telugu star had refused and Gajala had decided to take the extreme step, because of this. Actress Gajala has made it big in the Telugu industry by starring with heroes like Jagapati Babu. Her film Student No.1 with NTR Junior proved to be a big box office success in Andhra. She is currently starring in Balasubramaniam's film University, the audio-cassette release of which is on July 24 at Chennai. Makes you wonder if some of our stars are only 'brave' enough to face reel life... not the real one!
