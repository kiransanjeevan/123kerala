Titbits

 

Mohanlal in G S Vijayan's film: G S Vijayan has signed Mohanlal for his next film. Martin, the owner of Emil and Erik Company is producing this film. T A Shahid pens script for the film. Vijayan has directed films like Charithram, Aanaval Mothiram, Cover Story, Cheppadividya and Saphyalyam.

Sharat Kumar in Hariharan's film: Kollywood star Sharat Kumar plays an important role in Hariharan's Mammootty film Payyamvelli Chandu. The film has Mamta Mohandas as the heroine and would be an Onam release.

Kamal signs Mukta: Director Kamal has signed Mukta for his next film titled Goal. There are many new faces in the film shot in Ootty. Mukta has changed her name to Bhanu in Tamil and her latest Thamarabharani, directed by Hari released in Pongal is running in packed houses in Tamil Nadu.

Dileep in Thomson's film: Thomson, brother of Sibi of Sibi-Udayan team is for the first time directing a film. The central character is played by Dileep. Thomas entered the film industry as an assistant director to Jose Thomas in Mattuppetti Machan. He has also been assistant directors to Jose Thomas, Mohan Kapleri, Sandhya Mohan, Johnny Antony, Balukiriyath and Shafi. The film revolves around a mental hospital.
