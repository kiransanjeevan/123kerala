Preview - Finger Print

Jayaram is donning the role of a police officer in Satish Paul's Finger Print. He plays Vivek Shankar a soft spoken sincere cop who lacks confidence and self confidence but is now investigating a major murder case but is not able to piece it together.

Director Siddique who has written and directed blockbusters like Ramji Rao Speaking, Godfather, In Harihar Nagar and Friends is for the first time writing a script for a crime thriller to be directed by his assistant Satish Paul.

Indrajeet plays Krishna Varma, Vivek's colleague and a brilliant policeman who is very smart in investigation. How the two combine and solve this murder case is the basic story of the film. Gopika plays the female lead and the film also has Nedumudi Venu, Sai Kumar, Unni Sivapal, and Siraj.

The film is based on an investigative story with no songs. The film will be a February release. For Jayaram, the role of a police officer will give him a total image change. The shooting of the film started on December 7 in the picturesque location of Vagamon near Kolencherry. The film will be distributed by Central Pictures.
