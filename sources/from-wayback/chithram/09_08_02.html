Sreenivasan is back

You can't keep a good man down for long.

After a self-imposed go-slow of sorts, the hugely talented actor-director-writer Sreenivasan is back. In both big and small screens.

In the big screen, he is signed to play hero in two new films, one of which is Bhuvana Chandran's directorial venture. And in the small screen, the humorous Sreenivasan is anchoring a cinema show named Cheriya Sreeniyum Valiya Lokavum on Kairali TV.

"My hands are full now," says the man known for his sensitive and serious cinema. So what was he doing all these days, and why was he away from films? "I was not idling. I was working on various things. Though I did not take up any major assignments, I was always involved with cinema - if not acting or directing, at least thinking on it," he says earnestly. Thinking about cinema? Well, Sreenivasan is one of the few film personalities who takes his art and craft so seriously that he actually reflects on it deeply. Sreenivasan says quality cinema comes after sincere reflection. You have to have ideas. Then you must translate them into vision.

The TV show which he is hosting offers him plenty of scope for that. Understandably, it is a film-based show wherein he offers his sincere and straight-forward opinions on all things cinema, especially Malayalam ones. "I like telling them like it is. That is what I do in the show," he adds.

Sreenivasan will also interview film personalities for the show. Sreenivasan's formula for success in films is simple. Be it writing, acting or directing, it all boils down to observation. "If you can observe things, you will have a perspective. And from that flows everything". The other thing, which is equally important, according to Sreenivasan, is being good and simple. Though he does not claim to have that, everyone in the industry accepts that he has them. That is the reason he is successful in his own sort of way.
