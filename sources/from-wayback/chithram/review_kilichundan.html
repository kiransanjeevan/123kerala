Kilichundan Mambazham

Storyline
The best ever comedy team of Priyadarshan, Mohanlal and Sreenivasan trying to recreate their old magic with "Kilichundan Mambazham". The film is set on a Muslim Background where Mohanlal plays as Abdu who tries to run away with his boss Moythootty Hajji's third wife Amina. Mohanlal tries his best to get rid of his super human roles and molded the character of Abdu as a simple common man.

"Chekkutty Muthalali" marries his daughter Amina (Soundarya) to Moythootty Hajji (Sreenivasan) after knowing her relationship with fincancially poor Abdu (Mohanlal). Amina becomes Moythootty Hajji's third wife. Even after her marriage Abdu could not keep Amina out of his mind and he decides to go behind her. He searches down Amina and tries to meet her. Knowing his intentions Moythootty Haaji tried all possible ways to keep Abdu away from Amina. A fun-filled film with Mohanlal and Sreenivasan trying to recreate their old magic with Jagathy, Salim Kumar, Kochin Haneef and Ganesh Kumar.

Sreenivasan handles the script while Vidyasagar tunes for Beeyar Prasad's lyrics. The film is produced by Antony Perumbavoor.
