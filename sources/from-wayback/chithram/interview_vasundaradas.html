Vasundhara Das, On the real-life impact of reel roles

Your role in Filmstar is an attempt at serious cinema?

In Tanuja Chandra’s Filmstar, I play a character who, after being abused by her husband, kills him. I have known several women in real life who are exploited but have never managed to come out in the open. Portraying this role has made me delve into introspection and think hard about what women should do if they face such a situation.

What do you think women should do if they are exploited?

First of all, exploitation of women doesn’t happen only among uneducated people. Even educated women are subject to both mental and physical abuse. The government talks of equal rights for men and women but this is all about the issue of power. At the end of the day, women shouldn’t take things lying down. They have a voice to protest and should exercise their rights.

Have you done the playback singing for filmstar?

No, but I must say that the film has a very soulful bhajan.

Which are your other film projects?

I am working in a Malayalam film titled Vajram. It is about an actress who gets to encounter the darker side of the industry. Subsequently, I will start working on a film by Prakash Pelawudi. This film is inspired by real life.
