Review: Kanaka Simhasanam

 
The Rajasenan-Jayaram combo, who has given us some memorable films in the past, seems to have run out of steam. Their latest comedy Kanaka Simhasanam is a bitter pill, as story wise there is nothing new and the presentation is as old as the hills with some scenes lifted from old films.

Jayaram has a clichéd role, something he has done in so many films – a do-gooder with a heart of gold with a comedy streak who is trying to make both ends meet. Kanamkambaran (Jayaram) runs a drama troupe along with his wife Bharati (Karthika). The company is running at loss but the couple struggle to keep it going as their creditors are breathing down their throats.

One day, they strike gold when Udaya Varma (Janardhanan) and Ramakrishnan (Bheemnan Raghu) asks them to enact a real life drama. They want him to play Suryanarayanan the heir to the throne of the palace and childhood sweetheart of Seetalakshmi (Lakshmi Gopalaswamy) the princess. Kanakambaran takes up the challenge and also places his wife as the cook at the royal palace. Meanwhile a bad guy (Kiran Raj) who has an eye for Seetalakshmi lands up and how Kanakambaran sorts out all problems forms the rest of this predictable story.

Rajasenan has helped himself to various comedies in the same genres like His Highness Abdullah, Udayapuram Sultan among others. However the first half is tolerable but post interval the film turns predictable and is so boring that your heart goes out to Jayaram the actor who is totally wasted. But how long can a talented actor like him go on doing the same role over and over again? And Rajasenan’s direction and presentation is so stale and predictable that at a time when audiences are exposed to stylish films, such old-fashioned narration makes it terrible.

However, Jayaram lifts the film from falling flat with his screen presence and comedy timing. Suraj Venjaramood as a wannabe Minister does his usual Thiruvanathapuram lingo act. Stale stuff like Telugu dialect (Emandi??) to make Malayalees laugh (Ha! Ha! Ha!). Music by Jayachandran is average but are picturised like drama songs which is being booed by the audiences.
