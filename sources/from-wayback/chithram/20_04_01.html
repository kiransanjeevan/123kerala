Casanova dropped?

 

Malayalam superstar Mohanlal's big budget romantic action thriller Casanova, directed by Roshan Andrews, has been dropped as its original producers, the Confident Group of companies lost confidence and withdrew from the project after the lacklustre performance of Mohanlal’s latest big budget film Sagar alias Jackie - Reloaded, reports said.

The pooja of Casanova took place in January at Confidante Cascade, Bangalore and the shooting was to start from this month in Vienna, Austria. According to sources, all proceedings of the film have been indefinitely postponed.

 
 





 
 
The buzz, though unconfirmed, is that the Confident Group of Companies would no longer be producing the film. Some other sources indicate that Casanova has only been postponed on account of the delay in visa formalities. It is also heard that Casanova would be shot in Dubai, instead of Austria.

Scripted by Bobby and Sanjay, the film has Tamil actor Arya debuting in Malayalam and five heroines, including Lakshmi Rai, Bhavana and Priyanka. R. Divakaran wields the camera while Alex Paul sets music. The action scenes will be choreographed by Hollywood's stunt coordinator Franz Spilhaus. Casanova was planned as Mohanlal's big 2009 Onam release on September 2.
