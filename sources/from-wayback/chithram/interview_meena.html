"Madhumathi is not me": Meena

Meena is a great survivor in the South Indian film industry. While her contemporaries have settled down to marital bliss or faded away, Meena still rules the box-office with her never-say-die-spirits. And recently, she looks young and stunning after she lost about 10 kilos. Her radiant smile is her greatest asset and you will be surprised when you hear her speak fluently in Malayalam. She came into the film industry as a child artist and made her debut in Malayalam as Suresh Gopi’s daughter in Santhwanam.

The actress is on cloud nine after the stupendous success of the recent Udayananu Tharam that has turned out to be blockbuster. An exclusive chat with her:

More films in Malayalam
I really want to do more films in Malayalam. But I am not able to do all the offers that come my way due to my commitments in Tamil and Telugu.

Udayananu Tharam
Madhumathi is one of the best characters that I have done so far. My phone has not stopped ringing since the release of the film as everyone says that my performance was very restraint and matured. Full credit goes to script writer Sreenivasan and director Roshan Andrews.

Madhumathi
My character, Madhumathi is a film actress in the film who goes through lots of stress and is a very insecure person. But luckily for me I am not like Madhumathi in real life. I never had to face similar situations in my life. I have a wonderful family and I am very close to my mother and have a set of good friends. If I am not shooting I chill out with them, go out for a movie and have a great time.

New films
Ranjith's Chandrolsavam is my next release. Mohanlal is the hero and the film is slated for a Vishu release.

Marriage
My parents are on the lookout for that suitable boy. What I look out for an ideal man is sincerity, humour sense and above all a wonderful human being.
