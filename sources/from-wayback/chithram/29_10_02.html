Tamil superstar Rajnikant's daughter to wed teenage sensation Dhanush

Tamil film superstar Rajnikant has agreed to give his daughter's hand in marriage to the latest Tamil cinema hearthrob, 22-year-old Dhanush.

“For sometime now, there were rumours in the media that I was planning to marry Aishwarya. Our families thought why not make the rumours real and organise the wedding,'' Dhanush said at a media conference here.

The grapevine has it that Rajnikant was initially opposed to the marriage of his 26-year-old daughter, Aishwarya, to Dhanush. But the lanky actor on Thursday said that the ``arranged marriage with consent of both of our parents'' would take place here on November 18.

Dhanush, who made his debut in Thulluvatho Illamai a few years ago, now charges over Rs 1 crore per film. He has had a series of hits despite his unconventional looks. His latest movie, Sullan, however, flopped at the box office.
