Priyamani denies rumours of marriage

 

The pretty Priyamani, who has just had a Tamil release in 'Ninaithalae Inikkum' after quite a long time, is in trouble again with the scribes. When rumours of her alleged link-ups with some of her co-stars surfaced some time back, Priyamani had openly told the scribes that "Whenever you write a news item concerning me, please get it confirmed with me and then write. This is my mobile number where you can always get in touch with me" and gave her mobile number to the scribes.

The rumours never seemed to die down and fed up with non-stop queries, Priyamani had reportedly changed her mobile number. Meanwhile, many Andhra-based film websites announced on Monday that Priyamani had 'secretly' tied the nuptial knot with Telugu hero Tarun at an undisclosed location. This obviously created waves in the Telugu film industry, where Priyamani is a name to reckon with.

 
 





 
 
When rumours of her alleged link-up with Prithviraj surfaced some time back, Priyamani was quick in dismissing those rumours. This time around, though, she is keeping mum. The link-up between Priyamani and Tarun were reported in the media several times in the past. Grapewine has it that Tarun reportedly presented a brand-new Innova Car recently to the pretty actress.

Priyamani hasn't yet denied the rumours of both the gift as well as the marriage. Would it make it true? This is the million-dollar question doing rounds at a hectic pace in Tollywood and Kollywood at present!
