Now Shakeela is dropping jokes

Shakeela is having the last laugh. And the joke is on us. The sex bomb, whose films were a matter of debate as much as they were a spectacle for front benchers, is having an image make-over in Telugu. And what is that change? Well, she is donning the garb of comedienne in Telugu. For one who, in Malayalam, shed whatever garb she had, this is indeed remarkable.

The vital change came with the successful Jeyam in which Shakeela played the mirthful role of Gnana Saraswathi. When Teja conceived that role for Shakeela, many felt that it was yet another of Teja's quirks. As it happens, he too seems to have had the last laugh. Now Shakeela is pencilled to play in two films in Telugu, and both of them are comedy roles. In E V V Satyanarayana's Totti Gang and Nagarjuna's Rowdy, Shakeela plays the roles that are aimed at tickling the funny bone. Shakeela herself is smiling at the change. The buxom actress says she enjoys this new kind of exposure. Of course, she understands when somebody like her is playing the comedienne then there are bound to be bawdy double entendre dialogues. "It is natural. I don't mind that," she has been quoted as saying. Interestingly, Shakeela herself is coming across as something of a natural in comedy roles. "I love funny characters. It is a good challenge to me," she says. Shakeela is not sure how long this new fixation will last. So far so good, she says.
