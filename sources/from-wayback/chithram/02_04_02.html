Janakan postponed

 

Janakan directed by N R Sanjeev, which has Mohanlal in a cameo and was to release on April 1 for the Easter weekend has now been postponed to April 8. It has Suresh Gopi, Biju Menon, Harishree Ashokan, Kaveri, and Jyothirmayi in supporting roles.

The highlight of Janakan will be Mohanlal in a cameo as Advocate Soorya Narayanan, who is famous for winning every case that comes his way. In the film he comes to the rescue of Suresh Gopi and others who have been framed up in a murder case.

 
 





 
 
Now only April Fool directed by Viji Thampy, a laugh riot is releasing on April 1. The movie tells the hilarious events that happen in the life of a businessman played by Siddique which leads to a piquant situation.

Jagadheesh, Biju Menon and Jagathy will appear as the hero’s friends. The film will also introduce a Bangalore based model ‘Nayana’ as the heroine.
