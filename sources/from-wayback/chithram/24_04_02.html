Titbits

 

Suresh Gopi does a cameo: Suresh Gopi will be seen in a cameo in Achante Makan, which has Vinu Mohan in a lead role. The film directed by debutant Girish has Kollam Thulasi, Indrans, Spadikam George, Prajusha, Manju Pillai etc. The shooting of the film will begin at the end of this month in Wayanad, Gundelpettu and Ernakulam.







Bhavana in Uzbekistan: Actress Bhavana is in Uzbekistan to shoot a song sequence for the Tamil film Jayam Kondante. The shooting of the film will complete after picturising the song of both Bhavana and Vinay.

 
 





 
 
Mammootty in Loumiere Films: Mammootty will play the central character in the film produced by Loumiere Films after the mega hit Katha Parayumbol. The film directed by M Mohanan has story, screen play and dialogues by Sreenivasan. The film will be released next year. After Rowdram, Mammootty and Renji Panicker join hands once again in the film named Kaduvakunnil Kuruvachan. The film will be a Christmas release.
