Review : Pattanathil Sundaran

Cameraman turned director Vipin Mohan’s Pattanathil Sundaran operates on the belief that Dileep, the ‘Sultan of slapsticks’ along with a comedy brigade can make the audience laugh! What if debutant Sindhu Raj’s script goes haywire at times, the director has been successful to make the film click.

Sundaresan (Dileep as a tenth standard failed youth) is a ration shop owner. But he gets married to Radhamani (Navya Nair) a postgraduate looking out for a government job. The marriage takes place due to the Chowa Dosham in their jatakam but before their honeymoon is over Radhamani gets a job as a clerk at the state secretariat in Trivandrum. She is overjoyed but a highly egoistic Sundaresan is not. Soon they move to Trivandrum and stay in Sekharan Pillai (Cochin Haneefa) house.

The trouble with Sundaresan is that he becomes suspicious of Radhamani and tries his best to see that she loses her job. All this leads to hilarious situations and soon Radhamani gets fed up and applies for a divorce. They start living separately and meanwhile Radhamani is pregnant and she is determined not to show the baby to Sundaresan who dots on children! Will Sundaresan get a job and will the couple unite forms the rest of the story.

The film is a cross between slapstick comedy and family sentiments. The script seems to have been reworked by the scriptwriter and director to suit Dileep's “new” image and hence the film stands somewhere between seriousness and silly buffoonery. Navya Nair is superb as Radhamani with her tongue-in-cheek dialogues and love-hate relationship with her husband. Dileep proves once again that with his split-second comic timings, that he is the undisputed king of comedies. Mohan Sitara’s music has been lifted from old Ilayaraja hits like Chinna Thampi. Go ahead and watch the film if you are a fan of Dileep and his laugh riots.
