Mohanlal turns sculptor

Variety is synonymous to versatile actor Mohanlal. He played the role of a carpenter in ‘Rasatantram’, and now, he is playing the role of a sculptor in a new film titled ‘Aakasha Gopuram’, Meera Jasmine plays his heroine in it.

The shooting of the film has already begun in London now that his other film 'Paradesi' with Kunhu Mohammed has been completed. He plays the role of a sculptor from Kerala, who goes to London and becomes a renowned world artiste. A major portion of the film will be shot in London as the script demands.
