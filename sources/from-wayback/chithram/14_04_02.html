Meera Nandan in Tamil

 

It's the turn of Meera Nandan to migrate to Tamil films after Nayantara, Meera Jasmine, Asin, Bhavana and Bhama. Meera's debut film Mulla with Dilip and directed by Lal Jose is doing good business. She will be seen in Valmeeki, directed by Anantha Narayanan, an assistant of director to Shanker. It is a love story and the hero of the film is Hari, son of Ilayaraja's brother R D Bhasker. But, as she does not want to mess up her early career, Meera's very careful in accepting projects. She says, "Valmeeki is a heroine-oriented story, so I've decided to accept it."

 
 





 
 
Valmeeki, produced by Vikadan Talkies, is likely to begin shooting by May first week in and around Chennai. The music direction is by Ilayaraja and ace cinematographer Azhagappan will wield the camera for this film.
