Five movies for this Vishu

 

As usual with the most important release season of the Malyalam film Industry; during this Vishu also five of the best films from all the important stars in the industry are coming to hit the big screens.

The first to grace the theatres will be ‘Sagar Alias Jackey-reloaded’ a sequel to the eighties super hit ‘Irupatham Noottandu’. Directed and Cinematographed by Amal Neerad, this stylish action drama will grace the release centers by the 27th of March.

Lal creations will follow this big movie with their release of 'Hariharnagar-2’ - a sequel to In ‘Hariharnagar’ by the 2nd of April. The movie directed by Lal will have all the important heroes of the original, but in a new avatar. Lekshmy Rai is the heroine of this movie planned as a hilarious entertainer.

Suresh Gopi will have his ‘IG’ - an investigative drama, with director B. Unnikrishanan for the season, while Dileep is trying in every way possible to get his movie with veteran director Faazil ‘Moses D Samuel’ on theatres for the Vishu holidays.

 
 





 
 
As per the latest reports Mammootty will have two releases in the same month. His bilingual ‘Vande Mataram' is already over and will grace the theaters as a Vishu release. The movie directed by T Aravind featuring action king Arjun as the other lead hero will be released with the title ‘Aaruvadai’ in Tamil.

The second Mammootty flick is the special effects movie ‘Pattanathil Bhootham’ in which Mammootty is playing a double role. The movie will in all probabilities will grace the release stations by the end of April or by the first week of May.

Since the movie demands a lot of post production works and special effects works, the exact release date of the movie is not yet out.
