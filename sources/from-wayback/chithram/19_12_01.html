Titbits

 

Prithviraj in Joshi's film: Sachi and Sethy pens script for Joshi's new film named Robinhood which has Prithviraj in the lead role. After the success of Chocolate, Prithviraj has emerged as the hottest rising star in Malayalam films. It was Sachi and Sethy who penned for Chocolate also. The shooting of the film will begin in April. The film is produced by Santha Murali and P K Muraleedharan for Anandavision.

Kavya in Kalabhamazha: Kavya Madhavan plays the lead role in Kalabhamazha, scripted and directed by P Sukuram. The pooja ceremony of the film was recently held at Eline Media Theatre in the capital city. Minister C Divakaran was the chief guest and Gokulam Gopalan lit the traditional lamp. The profit of the film would be given to a charitable ogranisation Lopamudra Charitable Trust functioning in Palakkad.

 
 





 
 

Remake of Ishtam begins: The shooting of the remake of Ishtam in Hindi has begun in Goa. The film directed by Priyadarshan has Akshaya Khanna in the lead role. The film Genelia has the heroine. Other casts include Paresh Rawal, Om Puri etc. It has been reported that Malayalam actress will have an important role in the film.

Dhasavatharam on Feb 12: Kamal Hassan's big budget film Dashavatharam produced by Oscar Films will release on February 12. Kamal appears in ten roles and has Asin and Mallika Sherawat as heroines.
