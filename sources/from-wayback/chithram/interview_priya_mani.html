"All actors are talented and natural": Priya Mani

The Bangalore based Priya Mani is hot among producers in Malayalam. She has the plain Mallu looks and has signed with two big directors and top heroes. Her first film will be Satyam with Pritviraj directed by Vinayan and with Dileep in Lal Jose directed Albhuthavilakku.

Where do you hail from? Are you a Kannadiga?
My roots are in Palakkad in Kerala though I grew up in Bangalore. I am the granddaughter of Kamala Kailas the famous carnatic singer.

How did you enter films?
Through modelling. I had modelled for all major jewellery and textile showrooms and seeing my portfolio I was approached by directors.

But you don’t have any filmi background?
No actually my family was not too interested either. It was after I completed my 12 std that a call came from Bharathiraaja’s office. That was too tempting an offer to decline and I decided to give it a try.

Tell us about your first film?
The first schedule lasted for about 35 days and the songs were picturised in Switzerland. I went through lot of problems like catching a high fever that was later diagnosed as typhoid. I can never forget all that.

Have you seen Malayalam films?
Of course I have seen most of the good Malayalam films. Though I was brought up in Bangalore we are basically Iyer family from Palakkad and my mother is from Trivandrum.

Who is your favourite actor in Malayalam?
All are extremely talented and natural actors. I admire KPAC Lalitha for her versatility. Mammootty, Mohanlal, Dileep are my favourites.

How did you enter Malayalam film industry?
Actually I had signed Albhuthavilakku directed by Lal- Jose opposite Dileep but the project got delayed. Later I signed with director Vinayan for Satyam opposite Pritviraj. It is a police story where I have a meaty role. I play a modern girl in the film.

What are your forthcoming films?
In Tamil I have completed the first schedule of Balu Mahendra’s Athu Oru Kanakalam with Dhanush and Ullam.I have also signed a film to be directed by Teja in Telugu. But right now I am completing these two projects in Malayalam.

What are your favourites in life?
Music, dance,ice creams, chocolates, puppies and cats. It’s in that order (smiles).
