Actress Bhama doubting about glamorous roles

 

Actress Bhama with her innocent looks has captured our hearts with her debut flick 'Ellam Avan Seyal' in Tamil. But according to her, it's her 7th film for she is already a famous actress in Malayalam film industry. 'Nai Vaidhyam', 'Cycle', 'Colors', 'Kannirundru Madhuram' and other films. She feels to be luckier as Director Lohitdas introduced her on the screen.

Speaking about her experience while filming for 'Ellam Avan Seyal', Bama says that she was more nervous during the ragging sequence where nine girls force her to kiss a dead body. "I thought and prayed to God this must not happen to anyone in real life".

 
 





 
 
At present, the actress is busy with more Malayalam projects and assures she would complete them within a period couple of months and get back gearing up with Tamil projects. Since the family type of image is more favored on her, Bhama doubts whether looks of modern girl and glamorous roles would suit her.
