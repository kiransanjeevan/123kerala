Kerala Filmdom on a warpath

For all those who're waiting to know what's to happen after the Kerala film chamber decided to ban all film artistes who work or produce TV serials, here's the latest. The animosity has now erupted into a full-fledged war. Superstar Mohanlal has declared that it was for the audience to decide whether they should see his films or not. "If they decide that they will not see my films because I am appearing in ads and producing a serial, then I am willing to say goodbye. I have contributed in my humble way to the betterment of Malayalam cinema in the state and on the national level. I am the only producer who is still active after incurring a loss which is the highest that any producer has suffered. Each and every morsel that my family and I eat is because of the common man who sees my films. Let them decide," he says.

The other superstar Mammootty has also joined his colleague, in voicing his opinion. He said that it was foolish to think that TV serials with film artistes were the cause of films flopping. The Chamber taking arbitrary action, was not right. "We will continue to act in films and do serials. Let's see what will happen," he echoes. Jagathy Sreekumar the comedian says that he will act in dramas for Rs 500 but will not bow down to the Chamber. Shaji Kailas the hot-shot director is even more defiant. On July 1st, the cutoff date announced by the Chamber, he would be launching a mega serial for Surya TV. It will feature ten top film artistes. A full-scale defiance of the Chamber by the artistes might result in a split in the ranks of the Chamber. Mammootty has added, "Let them produce films with newcomers" and director Satyan Anthikad observes that discussions and seminars on crisis in filmdom are leading to more crisis. The artistes' reaction to the Chamber's decision was the outcome of a seminar organised by the KSFDC on how to solve the issue. Let's wait for the Chamber to react... stay tuned!
