Review: Kai Ethum Doorathu

'Kai Ethum Doorathu' is almost a typical Fazil film, but does not have the right ingredients to retain the viewers' interest for a long time. Produced by Fazil himself, under the banner Ammu International, the film elicits applause from the viewers, only when Mammootty, who does a cameo, appears on screen. 'Kai Ethum Doorathu' is supposed to be a launch vehicle for Fazil's young son Shanu, who however doesn't seem to appeal much to Malayalee filmgoers. The heroine Nikhitha, though cute, doesn't impress much as an actress. The film is rather slow-moving and lacks substance. However, it is definitely an improvement on  the 'crash-bang' flicks currently holding sway. Mammootty impresses as Advocate Gopi, while Revathy as Sushama's mother too does an excellent job. Siddique is well cast as Sushama's father Babu. All the others in the cast are just so so, including the lead pair Shanu and Nikhitha, plus Sudheesh, Cochin Haneefa, Rajan.P.Dev, Harisri Ashokan and Yadukrishnan. Songs penned by S.Rameshan Nair and set to tune by Fazil's favourite Ouseppachan don't seem to rise above average.

Storyline

Sachin Madhavan alias Sachchu and Sushama become intimate after they meet for the first time at a birthday party. Sachchu begins to love her, without knowing if she reciprocated. In the meantime, he comes to know that Sushama is to be married soon to US-based Dr.Kishore, a boy chosen by her parents. Sushama's mother Omana arrives to make the preparations for the engagement, but her father Babu doesn't turn up. Since Sushama feels that her parents were likely to divorce after her marriage, she revolts against her mother by accusing her of having an affair with Sachchu. This complicates matters. And consequently Sushama runs away with Sachchu to a distant place. It is at this stage that Advocate Gopi makes his entry with some shocking revelations.
