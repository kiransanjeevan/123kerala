Hastings is a south-eastern town of England. It is situated on the sea side with a lot of historical values. Malyalees from different parts of Kerala work here at different positions. Hastings Malayalee Cultural Association(HMCA) is a mlayalee association formed of those malayalees who live in Hastings area. HMCA is a non-profit making organisation which is mainly working to keep up the traditional and cultural values of the Kerala among the members. The association is registered with the Hastings Council and its running is purely based on its constitution.

As a start, the association had a colourful Onam celebration. Onam celebrations were held on the 23rd of September 2006 at Concordia Hall, Hastings. Pookkalam satrted at 9am and the whole days event came to an end at 6.30pm. Guests from Hastings Council were also present together with its members. Recommendation from our guest together with the media supportlocally and also from home definitely motivated HMCA.

Onam program started with Pookkalam. Colourful pookalam reminds everybody home and those Onam days in Keralam. Nilavilakku was lit by two elder mothers which denoted the beginning of the celebrations. Various competitions for children ladies and men were held. Seperate but exciting tug of war was the highlight among competitions. We had a fantastic Onam Sadya which was prepared by our own ladies at different houses. Visitors and guests highly commended about our traditional vegetarian Onam sadya. Executive committee for Hasting Malayalee Cultural Association was elected after the Onam sadya.

The afternoon session was mainly for stage program. Various kerala traditional items were performed on stage. Dances, Mahabali, solos, comedy dance, kitchen dance, and vallam kali are some of the items we had on stage. The stage program was concluded by a ganamela organised by HMCA.

Keralites live here had a lot of fun and immense enjoyment through the day.

HMCA is specially invited for a multi-cultural event on 7th October 2006 at Sussex Hall, Whiterock Theatre, Hastings.
