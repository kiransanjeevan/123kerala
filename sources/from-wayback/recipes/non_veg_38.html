Egg Salad
Ingredients:

Ingredients:

6 eggs (fully boiled)
2 boiled Potatoes
1 Capsicum 1 onion (thinly sliced into rings)
1 Tomato (thinly sliced into rings)
2tsp lemon juice
salt to taste
Black pepper to taste
Finely Chopped coriander leaves

advertisement

Method of Preparation

Cut eggs into halves. Take out the yolk and mash with boiled potatoes. Mix salt, pepper, lemon juice and chopped coriander. Fill eggs whites with the mixture. Serve the egg salad in a tray garnished with onion, tomatoes, chopped capsicum & coriander.
