Namitha arrives in Malayalam

 

Mohanlal's age is very much inversely in proportion to the ages of his heroines. The older he becomes, the younger his heroines get. These days, the number of heroines is also increasing. There was a time when he'd usually act with two heroines. Now, the number has risen to four!!! 'Kartika Thirunaal Karthikeyan' is the name of the new film that he is working in. DS Suresh Babu is the director of the film.

The film is going to have four heroines- Parvathy Melton (of 'Hello' fame), Padmapriya, Swetha Menon (who is becoming a regular in most of his recent films) and the bulky & voluptuous Namitha.

 
 





 
 
Namitha's role in the film hasn't been officially confirmed yet. If she accepts the offer, this would be her first film in Malayalam. Parvathy Melton was dead against working in Malayalam films in future. But it looks as if she too had decided to change her mind. The film's shooting begins in the first week of March 2008.
