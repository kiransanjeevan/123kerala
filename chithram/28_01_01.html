I hope Colours will be accepted: Dileep

 

Dileep is once again back to lead the top slot. With ‘Crazy Gopalan’ turning a grosser, things are looking fine for him again. Also his first outing as a producer with ‘Twenty 20’ has reaped great results with the film turning to be a blockbuster. This certainly hints that good time has come for Dileep. His eyes are now set on his forthcoming ‘Colours’. The film is directed by Raj Babu. Besides Dileep, the starcast of the film comprise Roma, Bhama, Vinu Mohan and Sharanya. Innocent, Harisree Asokan, Sai Kumar and T P Madhavan are also in the cast.

"I hope Colours will be accepted by the audience. My character comes after a while in the film and from then on the story takes a new turn. I sport a different get up for the film. I was earlier criticized for not having macho looks so I decided to give my own special features to the characters. It has been going on for a while now and in Colours too. I have worked on my looks to suit a naval officer," says Dileep.

The film has a military camp as the backdrop and was shot mainly in Ootty and Coonoor. “The story of Colours begins with the focus on Lt Col Rajalakshmi (Sharanya), a doctor at a military hospital. She has two daughters, Pooja (Bhama) and Pinky (Roma), who have contrasting natures. While Pooja is a matured and obedient girl, who has now joined the nearby school as a teacher, Pinky is quite a tomboy and lives life on her own terms. She anchors a show on a local TV channel. Their lives change drastically after Sanjaynath (Dileep), a naval officer and an old family friend get transferred to their place," says director Raj Babu.

 
 





 
 
Roma in the role of Pinky is very excited about her character. "I am in fact known as Pankan among my friends, as my character is boyish in nature. It's without doubt one of my best roles so far. It has lots of substance and its fun playing this bubbly tomboy," says Roma. She says that the title of the film has been derived from her show on TV called Colours-The Pinky Show, modelled on MTV-Bakra.

Saloo George is the cinematographer and the music is by Suresh Peters. The film is produced by Sunitha Productions and will release later this month.
