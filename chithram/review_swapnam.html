Review: Swapnam Kondoru Thulabharam

Rajasenan the master of making family entertainers is back after a break with Suresh Gopi in the lead with Swapnam Kondoru Thulabharam. It is a typical run of the mill story of two brothers, their love, sacrifice and jealousy.

Madhavan Thambi (Janardhanan) and Prabhavathy (Sreevidya) have two sons Vishnu (Suresh Gopi) and Aniyan kuttan (Kunchakko Boban). Vishnu is ten years elder than Aniyan and they dot on each other though they are like chalk and cheese. Vishnu is a soft-spoken, humble hard working ideal son, while Aniyan is brash, irresponsible and outgoing. He gets into trouble often at college and home and it is Vishnu who bails him out. Aniyan has a terrible inferiority complex and feels that his brother always outshines him everywhere. Vishnu gets the job of a college lecturer in Aniyan’s college and Ammu, another student (Sruthika) is madly in love with him. When Vishnu avoids her, she gets closer to Aniyan and he mistakes it for love.

At home Aniyan feels insecure and his jealousy towards his brother increases day by day. And one day when he knows that Vishnu’s marriage has been fixed with Ammu all hell break lose. He hatches a plan with his college mates to kill Vishnu. And what happens in the climax is to be watched in the theatre. Suresh Gopi as Vishnu and Kunchakko Boban as Aniyan are equally good. The two new heroines Sruthika and Nandana are fresh and have done their roles well. The music of Ouseppachan is melodious and suits the mood of the film. If you are looking for a clean family entertainer go ahead with your family and watch it.
