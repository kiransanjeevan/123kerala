Dileep's new assignments

After CID Moosa, Dileep once again turns to production. He will be producing T V Chandran's film Kathavaseshan under the banner of Grand productions. Dileep plays the role of an engineer named Gopinath and Jyothirmayi plays heroine. Jagathy Sreekumar too has an important role in the film. T V Chandran who directed films like Ponthanmada, Danny, Susanna and Padom Onnu Oru Vilapom, is for the first time directing a film with Dileep. In debutant director Jayasurya's film Speed for Swargachithra Dileep plays the role of a sportsman named Ajith and Bhavana plays his heroine.After Meesha Madhavan, Dileep and Lal Jose team up once again in Rasikan where Bharat Gopi's son Murali Krishna does the screenplay. Girish Puthencherry-Vidyasagar score the music. In December the shooting of Johnny Antony's Kochi Rajave with Dileep will start. Sibi-Udayan team is working on the script. Apart from this Dileep has taken the Cochin sector distribution rights of Jayaraj's Rain Rain Come Again. His brother Anoop looks after the production side.
