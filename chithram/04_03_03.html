Kerala State Film Awards 2003 announced

The Kerala State Film Awards for the year 2003 were announced by Jury chairman T K Rajeev Kumar. Nedumudi Venu (Margam) and Meera Jasmine (Padom Onnu Oru Vilapam, Kastooriman) have been adjudged the best actor and best actress.

Margam directed by debutant Rajeev Vijayaraghavan has bagged the award for the best film of the year while Sibi Malayil is the best director for Ente Veedu Appuvinteyum . The second best actor and actress were bagged by Siddique (Choonda, Sasneham Sumitra) and Roseline (Padom Onnu Oru Vilapam).

P.Jayachandran and Gayathri bagged the awards for the best male and female singers. Kalidas (Ente Veedu Appuvinteyum) won the best child artiste award. Venu (Margam) is the best cameraman. Best lyricist is Girish Puthencherry and music director is M.Jayachandran.
