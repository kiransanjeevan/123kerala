2007 - More Misses than hits

 

Most of the Malayalam heroines, except perhaps Kavya Madhavan, seemed to be concentrating on Tamil while non-Malayali actresses like Padmapriya and Vimala (Vimala Raman)continued to be active here.

The year marked the entry of many debutantes, in all fields of filmmaking, some of them making their presence felt, while most just fizzing off. On the technical front the people worth mentioning are cinematographers Manoj Pillai and Azhagappan, who have been making their presence felt in all kinds of films. M.J. Radhakrishnan too made his presence felt, especially in low-budget, arty films, but he did make his impact on the whole. Another person to be mentioned is Adoor Gopalakrishnan who made news with his latest Naalu Pennungal, an anthology of four short stories on celluloid.

 
 





 
 
2007 was a good year for film music in Malayalam. There were many catchy numbers like Kolakuz-hal viliketto... (Nivedhyam), Mutta-the mulle... (Mayavi), Mandarapoo- choodi (Vinodayathra), Muthumani (Big B), Chellathaamare (Hello), Ishtamalle... (Chocolate), Kaiyethum doore (Ekantham) etc. Vayalar Sharath Chandra Varma in the lyrics department and Alex Paul in the music department dominated the scene. Music director M. Jayachandra too was active throughout the year while Anil Panachooran (lyrics) and Biji Bal (music), both debutantes, struck magic with Arabikatha. The songs of Pranaya-kaalam too were good.

Summing up, 2007 is a film of hits and misses with no new big star coming up and uncertainties still rampant as to what kinds of films would be liked by the people. Anyhow there are signs showing that Malayalam Cinema is on the verge of change and there are also signs that in the new year fresh talents could make their presence felt and also that the superstars and others could team up with the younger stock to give better films. Let’s hope so!
