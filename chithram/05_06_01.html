Sobhana, back as Sobhana

 






National award winning actress Sobhana is back to Mollywood, playing herself. The actress who was seen in Mollywod a long time ago, will appear in a short cameo in the new flick 'Musafir' which is being shot entirely in Dubai. The movie will have Rehman, Mamtha and Bala in the key roles. Basically a musical action thriller, ‘Musafir’ will feature six songs by Ouseppachan, Balabhaskar, Shahbaaz Aman and M G Radhakrishnan.

 
 





 
 

The movie is shot at 150 locations that had never been featured in a Malayalam movie. Directed by Pramod-Pappan, ace scriptwriter Babu Janardhanan will pen the dialogues and screenplay based on a story by Suneer Hamza. The shoot for the movie is almost complete and will grace the theatres in a short while.
