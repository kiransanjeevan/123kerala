"I am very happy": Mohanlal

Superstar Mohanlal, is a happy man after the stupendous success of his Onam release Naran. At a press conference in Thiruvananthapuram, the actor said that he was fortunate to have worked with eminent directors, script writers and artistes. Excerpts

You must be on cloud nine after the stupendous success of Naran?
I am very happy that Naran has become a blockbuster, a rare thing these days. I thank the audience of Kerala for coming and watching Naran in theatres. I hope that the success of Naran paves way for many more hits in the future for Malaylam cinema.

What is your next film?
It is Blessy directed Tanmatra produced by Century Films who are making a come back into production after many years. It is an off-beat film laced with commercial elements. The story focuses on the present educational systems and Alzheimer disease. On the whole, it has the scope to be an academy film.

Are you doing Ramgopal Varma’s Sholay?
It is still in the pipeline. I had a first round of talk with Mr Varma and I am supposed to do the role that late Sanjeev Kumar had made memorable in the original. Anyway that project will take time.

But the story of Naran was as old as the hills Please comment?
Maybe… but it the presentation that matters at the end of the day. Joshi has done a great job and people liked it. The underwater scenes shot by Shaji were appreciated by everyone. The stunt scenes between me and Siddique in the climax were brilliantly shot at sets erected in Vismaya studio.

Your all time five favourite directors?
Late Aravindan, as I love all his films. Then Sibi Malayil, Priyan, Fazil and Joshi.

Have you stopped producing films?
After making that critically acclaimed Vanaprastham, I am yet to come across a good subject for my banner “Pranavam” to produce. Let someone come up with a good script and then we’ll see.
