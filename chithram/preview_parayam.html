Preview: Parayam

Raju is the personal manager of Dream World. His family includes father, mother, sister and a brother. His father Chandrettan who owns a workshop is a tragic hero of a love story. So, he wants his son to love somebody and get married and encourages him in all ways.

In the meantime, Raju meets a girl Sudha who works in Reliance Customer Care Centre and falls for her. Chandrettan notices the sudden change in his son and asks him about the affair and does whatever he could do for his son.

Raju who goes to the hostel to inform Sudha about his love was stunned to see the incident, which happened there. What happens after that shot in a humorous manner forms the rest of the story.

In this film named Parayam, Jishnu and Bhavana as Raju and Sudha is the third film they are seen together. Innocent as Chandrettan and also include Cochin Haneefa, Salim Kumar, Nedumudi Venu, Vijeesh, KPAC Lalitha and Suja Karthika.

Suresh Poduval pens the screenplay and dialogues for the story penned by Jafar. Mohan Sithara tunes music for the songs penned by Kaithapram. Cinematography is by Jain Joseph. Anitha and Sujatha Films will bring the film produced by David Kachappilli under the banner of Chinku Achu Cinemas to the theatres.
