Stars shining on this Gemini star

By the end of May, the Gemini star takes over. That is astrologically speaking.

So is the case in cinema. Kiran, heroine of the Tamil film Gemini, all set to make a mark in Malayalam.

And befitting her status tag as the Gemini star, Kiran's entry into Malayalam cinema is with aplomb. Kiran is to play the female lead opposite Malayalam superstar Mohanlal in Thandavam.

As far as breaks go, this is really big.

Kiran is a north Indian who got a lucky chance in Tamil. "It was unexpected. This one too is," Kiran says lifting her dainty eyebrows.

Directed by Shaji Kailas, Thandavam's shooting is on in full steam in Mannathala near Trivandrum. Kiran is obviously excited at the prospect of acting with Mohanlal. "He is well-known all over India. He is a class actor and a popular star too. To be acting alongside him in my very second movie is great," she says with obvious relish.

Thandavam marks the coming together of Shaji Kailas and Mohanlal after their mega success Narasimham. Shaji Kailas and Mohan Lal, friends for several years, are known to vibe well. They have always come up with what the audiences like and Thandavam
looks no different.

Interestingly, it is not Kiran alone who is making her Malayalam debut through Thandavam. The buxom Mumtaz, the reigning sex bomb in Tamil, is also making an appearance in the film.

There is also a strong presence of established Malayalam artistes in the cast. Murali, Lalu Alex, Jagathy, Innocent, Kalabhavan Mani, Maiyan Pilla Raju have also been roped in.

But the talking point has been so far Mohanlal and Kiran. "Obviously, I am very excited with this offer. My first film is a success. With Thandavam I would like to continue the good work," Kiran points out.

The stars seem to favour the Gemini actress so far.
