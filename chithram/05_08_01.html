Mani moves on

Kalabhavan Mani seems to have immense energy stored in him, as he keeps on doing action films in spite of unsuccessful attempts. His recent releases like ‘Ravanan’, ‘Athisayam’ and ‘Kissan’ went to the doom yet he is not giving up the grip. He still moves on with action films.

Mani is pinning all hope on his next film ‘Chacko Randaman’ to bring back his past glory. It is an action-packed film with lots of punch dialogues and he plays a dual role of grandfather and grandson. Jyothirmayi and Mohini are playing the female lead roles and Sunil Kariyatukara is directing it.

People in the industry are still happy with him, as the average budget of his films is just Rs.75 lakhs. So, an average show at the theatres is enough to recover the business. Moreover, the success of his film ‘Ben Johnson’ has made him hot among the B and C audiences.
