Innocent's biggest despair

On the screen, Innocent has played the 'father' to almost all the leading actors in Malayalam. But, there is one role he would love to play.

Innocent had played the father to heroes like Mammooty, Jayaram, Suresh Gopi, Mukesh, Kunhako Boban and even Jayasurya. The audience loved him in these roles. But, he is yet to play Mohanlal's father in any film.

In al the films that the two had acted together, Innocent had either played Mohanlal's uncle or father-in-law. Some believe that Mohanlal is hesitant about casting him as his father because he believes that Innocent has greater screen presence than he does. And, Mohanlal has occasionally portrayed both father and son roles himself. So, he doesn't want Innocent to play the role of his father.

While Innocent regrets the decision, there are many who believe that Mohanlal has lot more to lose because he didn't play Innocent's son in any film.
