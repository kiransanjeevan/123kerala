Titibits

 

Jagadhish in Bollywood: Jagadhish, who acted in Priyadarshan's Hangama, is making a re-entry through T K Rajeev Kumar's Gushti. He enacts a shop owner in the film, which is a remake of Malayalam film Mutharam Kunnu PO. Other casts include Rajpal Yadav, Om Puri, Nargis, Asrani, Manoj Joshi, Sharat Saxena and many others. The shooting of the film is fast progressing in Karakudy.

Two films for Radhika: Radhika, who was noticed after Classmates, has got two films in hand. One of it will be a Kamal film. Radhika will play the second lead in B Unnikrishnan's Mohanlal film Madambi.

 
 





 
 

Bhavana rejects offer in Hindi: Bhavana today rejected a role in the Hindi remake of Chinthamani Kolacase due to date problems. Sunny Deol will play the main role in the film. The actress is at present in Pollachi doing a song sequence for the Tamil film Jayam Kondaan.

Padma Kumar signs Jayasurya: Jayasurya is once again doing a film with Mammootty after Bus Conductor. They will be seen together in Parunthu, directed by M Padma Kumar and scripted by T A Razak. Jayasurya plays the role of Vinayan, who seeks the help of Mammootty (Purushothaman), after involving in an unaccounted money racket.
