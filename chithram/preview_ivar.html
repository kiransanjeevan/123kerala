Preview: Ivar

T.K.Rajeev Kumar is coming out with yet another film Ivar on underworld dons. The story is all about underworld, goons and the mafia. There are two rival gangs in this film. Produced under the banner of Damor Cinema by Santhosh Damodaran (who had earlier produced Pakalpooram and Vaalkannaadi), Ivar attempts to portray the lives of those who belong to the underworld gangs.

The focus is a youth named Raghava Menon who joins the dreaded underworld gang led by Jose, known as 'Pambu' Jose. Jayaram plays Raghava Menon while Biju Menon is 'Pambu' Jose. Anil Murali plays another Ninja Hakkim. Bhavana is the lead heroine she plays the role of a DGP's daughter. Also in the cast are Siddique, Anoop Menon, Devi Ajith, P.Balachandran, Riza Bawa, Vinayakan, Janardhanan etc. Songs written by B.R.Prasad are composed by Sreenivas. Madhu Neelakantan is the cinematographer while Gireesh Menon is the art-director. Ivar is scheduled for release during Onam by Platinum Motion Pictures.
