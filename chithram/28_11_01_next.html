'I have always defied conventions', Vinayan

 

Have you felt uncomfortable being part of quite a number of controversies?

Of course, I have never really enjoyed being part of any controversy. Still, I can?t help raising my voice against wrong doings and hypocrisy. Regardless of the numbers who oppose me, I will continue voicing my opinions for things I thoroughly believe in.

If some people brand me as a rebel when I do that, I am least bothered about it. Of late, I have been quite vocal in highlighting the rights of the film technicians in my capacity of the chairman of MACTA and this has landed me into some controversies as well.

'I cannot ignore genuine issues'

What is your equation with other organisations in the industry, especially with the actors? group, AMMA?

We all are in good terms now. A common forum involving the various sections in the industry has been formed and I have been nominated as the chairman. We all had differences of opinion at some point or the other but finally, I consider this a recognition for the honesty in my efforts.

What is your opinion about the dubbed versions of films which are finding amazing success at the box office here?

It was me who raised the issue of ignoring the small budget films. Now the dubbed films are posing a great threat to the industry here. The Telugu films, which are being dubbed to Malayalam, are high budget ones, and we cannot always match the opulence or the richness in frames.

Moreover, when the other language industries never allow Malayalam films to be dubbed and shown there, why should we continue supporting this?

People who are hell bent on making a fast buck without thinking about the future of the industry are behind this. Not to forget that all this happens when our own films are finding it difficult to get theatres here.

We are all set to raise this issue on a more serious level and who knows, it could very well be the next controversy that I am involved. But I cannot ignore genuine issues.

'I love my life and enjoy every moment of it'

Why is it that your films create extreme opinions, in the sense that there are quite a number who enjoy them and another section who share an opposite view? Some of the fans associations are against my films. They start spreading the news that my films are failures even before they get released. It's a kind of an organised effort but I usually ignore such propaganda. I don't have any fans associations of my own, yet I am happy that there is a certain group of people who think that my films are different.

If you could start your life afresh now, how different it could be?

(Laughs) I love my life and enjoy every moment of it. I would like to live exactly the way I am doing right now.

 
 





 
 

Do you regret making the film Superstar, which has probably antagonised Mohanlal?

Not at all. Superstar was based on a comic subject, where the life of a person having an uncanny resemblance to a superstar was narrated.

It was based on certain true incidents which happened in the life of a person whom I know personally. The film had its own share of emotional moments as well.

But the real problem happened when some sections alleged that I was trying to bring up an actor who resembles Mohanlal. I have never understood how such a thing is possible. This issue was clearly blown out of proportion and let me make it clear that what I tried then was to tell a simple story.

Finally, there have been reports of a forthcoming film that you will direct, with Amitabh Bachchan as the hero.

I am planning a horror comedy and one of my producers has said that he has Amitabh Bachchan?s dates. I am not sure about how things will shape up as yet. If things happen well, it could happen next year but it's too early to comment on all of this.

I am also planning another film in Tamil, with Karthi (of Paruthiveeran) as the hero.

Source: Rediff.com
