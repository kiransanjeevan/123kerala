Phantom Paylee

'Phantom', produced by Dr.V.S.Sudhakaran Nair under the banner of Aparna Silver Images doesn't come up to the mark. You don't get what you expect from Biju Varkey, the director of 'Vaachaalam' and 'Devadasi'. But still, at certain points, the film succeeds in getting the audience's applause. And that perhaps indicates that this Mammootty-starrer could turn out to be a hit. Mammootty is well cast as Pylee. Malavika, who does the female lead, doesn't have much to do. Nishant Sagar as Jose and Nedumudi Venu as Gangadharan Pillai do justice to their roles. Manoj.K.Jayan as the villainous police officer Sebastian does his part very well. Cochin Haneefa as Devasya, Innocent as the local priest and Bindu Panicker as Maria are all excellent in their respective roles. However, Lalu Alex fails to impress as Dhimdhi Mathai. Out of the lyrics penned by Gireesh Puthencherry and set to tune by Deva, "Viral thottaal viriyunna penpoove..." is somewhat hummable. Biju Viswanath's camerawork, Sreekar Prasad's editing and Kanal Kannan's stunts are the highlights of 'Phantom'.

Story
Pylee, better known as Phantom Pylee is a stunt-actor in Bollywood. Pylee reaches Pallikara village to spend some time with his younger brother Jose. But Jose is not there as he has gone for a photography competition. Anyhow, Pylee stays in the village and mingles with the people there. The main issue concerning Pallikara is a granite quarry run by a man called Varadaraja Perumal. The quarry is a threat to the village and the whole locality itself. It could destroy a dam situated there. A group led by the Gandhian Gangadharan Pillai and others stage a protest against the quarrying and that leads to clashes. Pylee intervenes and things take a new turn.

Cast
Mammootty, Nishant Saagar, Bindu Panicker, Nedumudi Venu, Manoj K Jayan, Malavika
