Quick Chat with Manya

She's done about half-a-dozen films in Malayalam. But she's still referred to by many as the 'Joker'-fame heroine. That's Maanya, whose film 'Joker' directed by Lohitadas, catapulted her into star cadre. Manya, a native of Bangalore, began her career in modelling and got her first break in 'Deva', directed by Y.V.S.Chaudhary. The film turned out to be a hit and she turned into a sensation overnight. She then acted in a few Telugu movies like 'College', 'Ganapathy', 'Sivanna', 'Bachelors', 'Love' etc. It was during this time that cinematographer Venugopal and associate-director Blessy were looking for a new face to be launched in a new film by noted director Lohitadas. After a screen test, Manya snagged the female lead opposite Dileep in Lohitadas' 'Joker'. 'Joker' made her popular in Kerala, after which she acted in 'Vakkaalaththu Narayanan Kutty', 'Rakshasaraajaavu', 'Singari Bolona' (not yet released), 'One Man Show' and quite recently 'Kunjikoonan'. She also did a dual role in a tele-film titled 'Kanmani' beamed recently on Asianet. Manya also starred opposite Jayaram in the Tamil film 'Naina' directed by Manobala. She has won the Kerala Film Critics' award for her performance in 'Joker'. Here's details of a quick chat with Manya ...

Q: How was it to perform in 'Joker', your first film in Malayalam?

A: To be frank, I just love the character in the film and I feel that it's perhaps the best role I have played till date. Of course, I overacted a bit in the beginning. But Lohi sir (Lohitadas) interrupted and gave me proper guidance. And I slowly picked up. I am forever indebted to Lohi Sir. My co-stars Dileep, Bindu Panicker and others helped me a lot. I was feeling a bit homesick, but they helped me get rid of the feeling. And Bahadur'kka (the Late Bahadur) whose last film was 'Joker', encouraged me a lot. He even used to jokingly remark that if I agreed to change into a Muslim and change my name into Suraiyya, he'd perhaps marry me. Anyway, my character in 'Joker' was really lovable. In some places I have done my best, and in some other places, I feel I could have done better.

Q: What about your other Malayalam films?

A: I have been an ardent fan of Jayaram ever since I saw 'Thenali'. Hence I was thrilled when I got to act opposite him in 'Vakkaalaththu Narayanan Kutty'. The main attraction of the film was the song "Vaanavil…" which was done without any choreography. The director T.K.Rajeev Kumar left it to us to do the dance as per our wish and surprisingly it turned out to be quite satisfactory. 'Rakshasaraajaavu' was a film in which the heroines did not have much to do. And I was just one of the heroines, along with Kavya Madhavan and Meena. Anyhow, I did love working with Mammookka (Mammootty) and director Vinayan Sir. In 'Singari Bolona', I've done a good character along with Lal and Nishant Sagar. Hope the film will be released soon. 'One Man Show' was fun. I was already very close with Samyuktha Verma after doing the Alukkas Gulf show together. We were buddies already and so working together was fun. Director Rafi was very friendly. 'Kunjikoonan' has given me a chance to work with Dileep once again - he is one of my best friends in the industry. Director Shashi Shanker is good and Navya Nair too has become a good friend.

Q: Looks like you are very active on the Malayalam screen? Do you watch Malayalam films?

A: Yes, after entering the Malayalam industry, I have started watching Malayalam movies. The first one I saw was 'Ennu Swantham Janakikutty'. I also saw 'Daya', 'Thooval Kottaaram', etc. I like Manju Warrier and I adore Sharada Auntie.

Q: With you being a Kannadiga, don't you get films in Kannada?

A: Yes, only once. I got the offer to act in the remake of the Malayalam film 'Niram'. But I could not accept the offer since I was quite busy then. Of course, I get a lot of letters from my admirers in Karnataka.

Q: Any plans to shift to Bollywood?

A: I take what God gives me. I don't want to be over ambitious. I feel that if we are over ambitious we may have to make compromises. I want to lead a life that will satisfy me. I would rather marry, settle down and then do selective films, than go after roles.

Q: What is your ultimate ambition as an actress?

A: To be recognized by all as a good person. 'Manya' means 'respect' and that is what I want to earn in life. I'd rather do a film for the sake of challenge than for an award. I am not at all after awards.

Q: Could you tell us what your dream role is like?

A: Of course, I'd like to do a role like Sreedevi's in 'Sadma' (I have seen the film 50 to 55 times). I'd like to get roles like Nargis' in 'Mother India', Hema Malini's in 'Seeta Aur Geeta', Nutan's in 'Sujata', Madhubala's in 'Mr & Mrs.55' and Madhuri's in 'Pukaar'.

Q: What are your hobbies?

A: Cooking, gardening etc. Let me relate my very funny first experience in gardening. My father got me some flower seeds. I dug deep in the soil, some 4 to 5 feet, and then I put the seeds in and covered it with mud. I watered it for three months but nothing happened. It was much later that my father taught me how to sow the seeds. I am also a serial-buff. If at home, I spend everyday from 8.30 to 11 p.m. watching Star Plus. I enjoy watching 'Kyunki Saas Bhi Kabhi Bahu Thi'. I see films too. I enjoyed seeing 'Sixth Sense' and 'Stuart Little'.
