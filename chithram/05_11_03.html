Musical Chairs in Malayalam Films?

* The unthinkable is actually happening. Some producers are supposed to be replacing superstar Mohanlal with newcomers. Agustin Jude Creations' 'Mizhiranduillam' directed by Renjith was initially supposed to star Mohanlal, but later, Indrajith replaced Lal. The heroine is Kavya Madhavan.

* While Indrajith is believed to be replacing Lal in 'Mizhiranduillam', his much-in-demand brother Prithviraj is buzzed to be replacing Lal in Badran's 'Swarnam'. This film also has Kavya Madhavan as its heroine. The film is being distributed by Shogun with camera by S Kumar.

* On the heroines front, Kavya and Navya were supposed to be the leading ladies in Mohanlal's film 'Mr Bramachari' to be directed by Thulasidas and produced by Aroma Mani. Now buzz says that Meena is replacing Kavya in the film and Navya has no dates since she is currently acting with Dilip in 'Kalyanaraman'.

* Hit team-ups are currently happening in the industry. Producer Prem Prakash who produced 'Kooduevide' and 'Akash Dhooth' is making a comeback film with director Sibi, who incidentally directed these hits. Jayaram and his son Kalidasan are coming together again after 'Kochu Kochu Santoshangal'. Bobby-Sanjay, the two sons of the producers, are the script writers. Music is by Ouesphachan and the heroine is yet to be finalized.

* As everyone knows, the Jayaram-Sathyan Anthicaud team have given many hits like 'Vendum Chila Vettu Karyangal' (which is the latest). They are coming together again in 'Yathrakaruyude Sraadhakai' scripted by Srinivasan. Soundarya plays the female lead for the first time.

* Yet another hit team that is getting together is Mohanlal and Priyadarshan in Antony's film. Camera is by Ravivarman and music by Vidyasagar. The film is to commence shoot only next year.

* Remember Jayaram's film 'Thoovalkotaram' scripted by Lohitdas which was a hit? Well, the two are now together in a film produced by Kaliyoor Sasi. They say an elephant plays an important role in this film.

* On comebacks, we have the talented actress Manju Warrier creating speculation as to whether she may enter films again. The actress who settled down to wedded bliss with actor-husband Dilip, right at the peak of stardom, is now the mother of a little girl baby. She is buzzed to be ready to make a comeback in a film starring husband Dilip, produced by brother-in-law Anoop to be directed by a debutant. The film which is believed to be titled 'CID Moosa', may mark the beginning of the second innings for the actress, who, they say, will be acting only in films starring husband Dilip. Remember Hindi actress Bhagyashree of 'Maine Pyar Kiya'-fame who insisted she would act only in films starring husband Himalay? Hope Ms.Warrier's comeback doesn't suffer the same fate!

* Speaking of clinger-ons, the best one going is Suresh Gopi. The star, who's buzzed to be out-of-work with no takers for his films, is still holding on. They say, he recently received an offer to act in 'Swapanam Kondu Oru Thulabharam' produced by Hash Bush. The only catch was that it starred tenny-bopper Kunchacko Boban in the lead. But Suresh is said to have sportingly agreed to do a 'significant' role in the film. Maybe his 'Swapnangal' (Dreams) will come true after this film!

Until then, let's just wait for the film to complete shooting and watch this comedy on the big screen!
