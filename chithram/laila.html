A dimpled beauty steps into Malayalam Films

When Laila smiles, the most visible thing in her face is that dimple. Small wonder then that the Tamil, Telugu and now the Malayalam screens have taken to her.

She has been paired opposite Dileep by the redoubtable Vinayan in his forthcoming movie War and Love. This big break in Malayalam with such a hotshot combination is testimony to her abilities.

"She is a compact actress. No frills. no fancies, but plenty of efficiency,’' says Vinayan. That coming from a man like him is indeed a high praise for Laila.

Not surprisingly, Laila is thrilled.

"To be acting in Malayalam itself is a happy feeling for me. To be praised by somebody like Vinayan sir makes it doubly so,' Laila gushes.

War and love is a dream project for Vinayan. Ditto for Laila. "I play the role of the daughter of a Pakistani jailer," she points out. In these sensitive times, it is a very touchy role. One wrong expression could derail the whole thing. "It has been very challenging. I love such challenges," she adds

Dileep plays the Indian Army officer who falls in love with her. "It is a movie for the times we live in. It is not about war, which we are hearing about daily. It is about love which we don't talk of," she says.

Laila, who is a qualified ballet dancer, has been moving up the popularity charts in Tamil and Telugu having acted with all the important names there.

"I guess I am lucky to have got my breaks. But I would consider those to be deserving if only I deliver a few hits," she says honestly.

Till then? Well, let them talk about her dimples!
