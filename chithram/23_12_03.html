Mammootty's daughter weds

Glitterati of South Indian film world, political leaders of all hues and corporate captains thronged the Pool Lounge o Hotel Le Meridien on Monday evening to bless the couple Rehan and Surumy, daughter of Mammootty and Sulu.

Rehan is the son of Waheed Ahamed Sayed of Chennai. The nikah, solemnised by Imam Muhammed Rafi Moulavi, was held earlier in the day.

Adding colours to the reception held in the evening were who-is-who of Malayalam cinema led by Mohanlal, Nedumudy Venu, Balachandra Menon, Biju Menon, Mala Aravindan, Mukesh, Jayaram, Jagathy Sreekumar, Saikumar, Tilakan, Manoj K Jayan, Maniyan Pillai Raju, Kaviyoor Ponnamma, Jyothirmayi, Tamil stars Vijay, Ajith and others.

Top leaders including K Karunakaran, Pinarayi Vijayan, C K Padmanabhan, Kadannappily Ramachandran, M A Baby, Speaker Vakkom Purushothoman, Ministers M M Hassan and M K Muneer were also present.

KFDC chairman P V Gangadaran, DPI K R Viswambharan, Galfar Muhemmdali, Captain Krishnan Nair and other dignitaries were also present.
