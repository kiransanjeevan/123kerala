Preview: Bus Conductor

He is Zakir Hussain alias Kunjakka, the owner as well as the conductor of Maithri Bus in Eranad.

This bus service was first started by Kunjakka's father Abookka and his pal Madhavan. After his father's demise, Kunjakka took over the responsibility of the service. Kunjakka, an honest man, gives due respect to each and every passenger.

His family consists of two sisters - the eldest married and now has Selina, a heart patient whom Kunjakka is crazy about. When everybody forces him to marry - he was firm on his decision of marrying after Selina recovering from her disease.

Noorjahan Teacher who is in love with Kunjakka was ready to wait till his problems are over. But things transform when Suganthi comes in search of Kunjakka.

After Vesham, V M Vinu is bringing a clean family entertainer Bus Conductor which tells the life and times of an ordinary bus conductor. The script is by T A Razak.

Mammootty plays the role of Kunjakka. Mamta who was introduced by Hariharan in Mayookham enacts the role of Selina and Bhavana plays Suganthi. The role of Noorjahan teacher is played by Nikhita.

Jayasurya plays opposite Bhavana. Sanjeev Shankar wields camera for the film produced by Vaishak Rajan under the banner of Vaishak Films. M Jayachandran composes music for the songs penned by Girish Puthencherry.

The film also has Innocent, Augustine, T P Madhvan, Abu Salim, Sreeraman and many others. The shooting of the film is fast progressing in Ottappalam and surrounding areas. The film is expected to release during Christmas.
