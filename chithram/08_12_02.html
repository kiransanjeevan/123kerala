Mother of all multistarrers

 

The shoot of the biggest ever star cast assembled in the history of Malayalam films, is starting on December 7 in Kochi. The powerful Malayalam artists association AMMA presents in association with Grand Productions Twenty:20. AMMA as part of their fund raising is making the film in association with Dileep's `Grand Production` Twenty:20, directed by Joshy with the all members of the association acting free in the film!

Take a look at the impressive star cast which makes Twenty:20, the biggest ever multi-starrer in history of Indian cinema-

Male : Madhu, Mammootty, Mohanlal, Suresh Gopi, Jayaram, Dileep, Prithviraj, Mukesh, Manoj.K.Jayan, Balachandra Menon, Kalabhavan Mani, Indrajit, Jayasurya, Jagathy, Innocent, Harisri Asokhan, Salim Kumar, Cochin Haneefa, Manian Pillai Raju, Indrans, Captain Raju, Rahman, Siddique, Lal, Sai Kumar, Biju Menon and other male members of AMMA.

 
 





 
 
Female: Kaviyoor Ponamma, Sukumari, Meera Jasmine, Kavya Madhavan, Navya Nair, Padmapriya, Bhavana, Samvrutha, Vimala Raman, Kalpana, etc, etc.

The promo blurb says - "All stars in one screen!". The shoot of the film will be in one single schedule from December 7 to January 27 in around Kochi. There are four songs in the film written by Girish Puthencherry and tuned by Benny Ignatious. The story and script will be by Uday Krishnan- Sibi.K.Thomas and Camera by P.Sukumar.

A spokesperson of `Grand Productions` said: "Twenty:20 will be completed in one single schedule and will be the ultimate multi-starrer in India. All the actors will appear as characters throughout the film and be a part of the story. The film will release on March 14, as Summer special."
