Mythili for another biggie

 

Mythili who got into popularity following her title role in the ‘Palery Manickyam-Oru Pathira Kolapathakathinte Katha’ seems to be careful in selecting her roles. She had a number of projects around her after ‘Palery’, but this model turned actress insisted only on accepting the plum role that she had in the movie ‘Nallavan’ which will come up in a short while.

 
 





 
 
But of late she has also started shooting for the new movie ‘Shikaar’ opposite Mohanlal. She will play the role of lover of Balaraman, the character played by Mohanlal in the movie. Directed by M Padamkumar, the movie is getting completed at a good pace at Kothamangalam.
