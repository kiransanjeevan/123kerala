Padmapriya trying different genres!

 

Actress Padmapriya, who was last seen in the Tamil film Irumbu Kottai Murattu Singam, which also featured RaghavaLawrence and two other actresses — Lakshmi Rai and Sandhya — in the lead roles, is now working on her next project titled Gokulam, which is of a completely different genre. The film, which has Sharvanand of Kadhalna Summa Illa fame playing the lead along with her, is being directed by Chandra Siddarth. A source close to the film’s unit says that the film revolves around a couple with different backgrounds.

 
 





 
 
Discloses the source, “While Sharvanand, who plays a techie, hails from a village in the film, Padmapriya plays a city-bred girl who is of the view that cities are far better than villages. However, she happens to travel with Sharvanand to his village and once there, a series of events get her to change her opinion about villages. The events also strengthen her love for Sharvanand in the film.”

The movie, a sweet, romantic film, will have music by Anoop Rubbins. Camera has been handled by J Krishnan.
