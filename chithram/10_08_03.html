Mohanlal's Onam release: Thandavam

The Shaji Kailas-Mohanlal team has always spelt magic at the box office. Come Onam, the duo are getting ready to launch their next film 'Thandavam' with the title role of Kaashinathan being played by Mohanlal. In fact, whenever Mohanlal has teamed up with Shaji Kailas, the films have turned out to be maha-events at the box office. While the team's first film 'Aaram Thampuram' was a super-hit their second one 'Narasimham' proved to be one of the top-grossers in the history of Malayalam cinema. And 'Thandavam', which is the team's third film, is expected to work wonders too.

The person who perhaps pins the most hopes on 'Thandavam' is producer Johny Sagarika, whose earlier production-venture, the Mohanlal-starrer 'Chakram', was aborted mid-way, incurring heavy financial loss. Johny Sagarika, who is investing his trust in the Shaji Kailas-Mohanlal duo, is very happy that he received a complete and thorough script for 'Thandavam' well before the shoot commenced a few weeks ago.

One of the highlights of the film is the script by Suresh Babu. Though just one film old as a scenarist, Suresh Babu has already stepped into the limelight with 'Dadasahib'. This young scenarist with a journalistic background is perhaps the highest-paid debutante scenarist in Malayalam cinema. If in 'Dadasahib', Suresh Babu created a character than appealed very much to the fans of Mammootty, this time in 'Thandavam' he creates in Kaashinathan a character that will surely win the hearts of Mohanlal fans.

Thandavam, which is to be released this August, during Onam, tells the story of Kaashinathan, who, by virtue of hard work, rises high in life. He is a sort of a messiah for the people of Mithilapuri, his native village. At the same time, he is a revolutionary in his own way and is a dread to oppressors. Mohanlal plays Kaashinathan, while Kiran plays the female lead. Nedumudi Venu too plays a key role. Also in the cast are Jagathy Sreekumar, Jagadeesh, Manoj.K.Jayan, Saikumar, Saadique, Lalu Alex, Manianpillai Raju, Janardhanan, Vijayakumar, Salim Kumar, Vineetha, Mumtaz, Bhavani and others. 'Thandavam' has cinematography by Sanjeev Shankar, lyrics by Kaithapram, music by M.G.Radhakrishnan, art-direction by Boban and editing by L.Bhoominathan. The film is being shot in Thiruvananthapuram, Pollachi and Chennai. 'Thandavam' would be brought to the theatres by Sagarika Release.

So, let's wait to see if Shaji Kailas and Mohanlal repeat their magic in August... with 'Thandavam'.
