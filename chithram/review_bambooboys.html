Bamboo Boys

Storyline
Four tribal youth, belonging to the 'Mulayadi' tribe, travel long distances through the jungle and finally land up in town to fetch a doctor to treat the ailing wife of their chieftain. These four youth - Olanga (Kalabhavan Mani), Nakki (Harisri Ashokan), Makku (Cochin Haneefa) and Themba (Salim Kumar) - wish to marry Kudukki (Priyadarshini), the daughter of their chieftain. And since the chieftain had promised that he would give Kudukki in marriage to the person who fetches the doctor, the four enthusiastically set off in search of a doctor. But when they reach the city, it's an entirely new world for them. They are at a loss. It is in the meantime, that they get entangled with a criminal gang which is engaged in some sort of spy work. Varkey (Jagathy Sreekumar), a police officer who is also a television artist, Haritha Kumari (Bindu Panicker), a poetess and nature lover and the members of the criminal gang make their entry into the lives of these tribal youth and things begin to hot up. Haritha Kumari is keen on helping the tribal youth while Varkey wants to grab the criminals with their help and thus save his job. The criminal gang follows the youth, since they have put a CD into the bamboo figurine carried by the tribals as a symbol of their deity. After many funny incidents, the story reaches a climax.

Review
'Bamboo Boys', produced by V.H.M.Rafeeque under the banner of Sealine Movies and directed by Ali Akbar, is a laugh riot. Ali Akbar has always had a special skill in handling humour and he delivers his best in 'Bamboo Boys'. Kalabhavan Mani, Cochin Haneefa, Salim Kumar, Harisree Ashokan, Jagathy Sreekumar and Bindu Panicker do full justice to their respective characters. Of the songs in the film, "Karuththa kozhi…" and "Eninnale choppanam…" are folk tunes and may appeal to lovers of folk music. The director Ali Akbar has done a good work of the script, trying his best to create lively scenes. Shashi Perumaanoor has done a good work of the art-direction. In short, 'Bamboo Boys' is a slapstick comedy, worth watching.
