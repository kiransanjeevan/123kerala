Shaji Kailas-Mohanlal team once again

This team has always spelt magic at the box office. The duo are getting ready to launch their next film which is expected to begin next year. In fact, whenever Mohanlal has teamed up with Shaji Kailas, the films have turned out to be maha-events at the box office. This film will be entirely different from the earlier films. Shaji is trying to give a face-lift to Mohanlal with something different. Shaji Kailas is busy doing films in Telugu. At present, Shaji is doing a film in Tamil named Jana with Ajith in the lead role.
