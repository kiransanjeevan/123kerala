Shahid Kapur raves about Rani Mukherjee

 

He may be the star of one of the best films to have released in Hindi this year - that has done exceptionally good business in the country and abroad - but he is still answering questions about ex-girlfriend Kareena Kapoor. He was asked recently if hewas still in touch with Bebo, to which the actor replied: "Not at all." But curiosity about the actor's love-life has escalated since the two broke up, with him being linked up with Kismat Konnection co-star Vidya Balan, tennis star Sania Mirza, and now Kaminey co-star Priyanka Chopra. When asked if he was planning to stay a bachelor or looking for love, the actor told a news channel: "According to the media, I have a 'buffet' of girls before me." He adds, "Let me find the girls first."

So, will he prefer to be a 'kamina'? They get the girls, after all, don't they? "Yes, they do, but they don't get settled," he said. "I would rather be 'sharif' and get settled."

 
 





 
 
But that hasn't stopped him from raving about his next co-star, Rani Mukherjee. The two are set to be seen in Dil Bole Hadippa, which releases this September. According to Kapur, "Rani has never looked so good in the near past."

Speaking about the experience of working with Rani, Shahid said, "She is fun to work with and I had a great experience with Rani. I remember, in the song where she dressed like a Punjabi munda and had to do her own choreography, she did some naagin steps which were quite amazing."
