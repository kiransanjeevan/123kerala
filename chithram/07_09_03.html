Stars on eclipse

Perhaps, it is in the stars. Or rather, it is not in the stars.

Well, these are not the most auspicious times for top stars in south India. In the neighbouring Tamilnadu Baba has bombed spectacularly at the box office leaving a question mark over the seemingly infallible Rajnikanth. His arch-rival Kamal Haasan too is no better shape. His Panchatanram was just about average, and the actor's personal life is in a mess.

In Malluwood things are no different. Mammooty's reign at the top seems to have ended as none of his recent films have fared well. What of Mohanlal then? Well, there were high hopes that his Thandavam, released for this Onam, would have a good run as Mohanlal was anxious to put behind the failures of Praja and Onnaman. As Thandavam was directed by his trusted director Shaji Kailas, everyone was sure that the glad news was ready to be heard and the cash boxes would tingle. But what is ringing instead is alarm bells as Thandavam too has bitten the dust. It is hardly any consolation for Mohanlal that none of the releases for this Onam could be classified a hit.

Most of the industry watchers are baffled and saddened by the turn of events. "These are indeed tough times. This Onam it has been particularly hard as all the films have got a lukewarm opening. Mohanlal's Thandavam has been classified a failure while Mammooty's Karmegam in Tamil and his cameo appearance in Kaiyethum Doorathu have not exactly set any river on fire," says Aiyaappan Pillai, a leading distributor.

The only film which seems to have elicited some kind of response is Rajasenan's Nakshathrakannulla... starring among others the hugely hyped Prithviraj. Though the film, the first release for Sukumaran's son Prithviraj, is just about passable, it is the only one that has got a decent opening. Fazil's Kaiyethum Doorathu has been described as too slow and tepid. His son Shaanu who has made his debut in that film though impressive has not been able to carry the film on his young shoulder.

Leading distributors feel that whenever top stars' films fail there is bound to be a chain reaction below as is evidenced now. Points out Aiyaappan Pillai: "It is universal. It is the top stars' performances that are symptomatic of an industry's health".

In the event, pink is not a shade that one would associate with Malluwood now. Now that Thandavam has fizzled out, the optimists and the die-hard Mohanlal fans are looking up to his forthcoming movie Chaturangam, directed by Madhu.

It is Chaturangam indeed for the entire industry.
