Review: Mambazakkalam

Yet another film on the lines of Baleetan. Joshi known for his films with heroes as supermen takes a different track of showing human beings. Though the story is nothing new the treatment is fresh.

Venu a school headmaster values his prestige more than anything and refusing to bow down before money hangs himself in the school. Chandran the eldest to run the family goes off to gulf. Years later he comes back as a rich guy. With his money everybody in the family is settled in life. Only he is alone. Forty years and no one even asked him whether he wanted to marry. He comes across the old flame Shobana. She is married and mother of a kid. Her hubby is a criminal in jail. Old romance is rekindled. This creates friction between Chandran and his kith and kin.

Mohanlal as Chandran and Shobana as the old flame give a very good account. The supporting artistes like Sudhish, Mani and Harisree Asokan are an asset to the film. Cinematography and music are also the plus points in the film. Shammi Thilakan's character has an abrupt ending and Thiagarajan's stunts are a minus.
