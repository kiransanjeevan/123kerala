Meera Jasmine shines in Kannada

 

Whenever a powerful author-backed role is written in any language, directors find Meera Jasmine to be the right choice.

Meera has matured and now she has set eyes on Kannada, where she has completed director M.S. Sathyu's off-beat film Ijjodu produced by Reliance Entertainment, where she plays the role of a Devadasi.

In fact the veteran director had chosen Meera after watching her performance in the Malayalam film ‘Perumazhakkalam which was selected for the Indian Panorama 2005 for which he was a jury member.

Said Meera in a recent interview: "I want to do at least one good film every year. Fortunately, that wish has been fulfilled till date. I need that one role to keep me going till I get the next good one"

 
 





 
 
In Ijjodu, Meera plays Chenni, a lower-middle class girl who practices the Devadasi tradition. Initially she feels bitter about it but later thinks of it as her duty in order to safeguard her village from danger leading eventually to a disaster.

Adds Meera: "The story is essentially incident driven and portraying Cheeni was definitely the biggest challenge that I faced". The film was shot in Sync sound, where Meera did spot dubbing and also learned classical dance.
