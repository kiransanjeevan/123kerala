Dileep is still hot

Film offers go dry for some actors even if a single film fails to hit off well, but Dileep is an exception. His latest release ‘Don’ managed to make just a decent outing but he is still hot among the producers. The fact is, he has got a few promising films on hand.

He has a few films in his kitty like Lohitha Das's film titled ‘Chakkaramuthu’. He is going to act with Mukesh in director Sathyan Anthikad's new project, which is slated for release this Christmas. Then he would get into another film to be directed by Lal Jose. The duo had earlier worked together in films like ‘Meesa Madhavan’ and ‘Chandupottu’. The list of films doesn’t end here. He is also slated to act in a new film to be directed by Pradeep Nair of ‘Oridam’ fame.

Here is a good chance for the actor to reach the top with so many promising films on hand. Keep going Dileep!
