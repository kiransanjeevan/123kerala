Time for Mammootty to iron clothes

It looks like versatile actor Mammootty can handle all kinds of roles under the sun with equal ease. He has excelled enacting various roles in the past, and this time, he is going to play a character, who earns a living by ironing clothes in his next film titled ‘Karuththapakshikal’.

The film is all about Murugan, who lives by ironing the clothes of other people. He lives in a slum and speaks a mix of Malayalam and Tamil, as he comes from Tamilnadu. He looks after his children Mayil, Azhagappan and Malli after the death of his wife. He dreams to revive the eyesight of his daughter Malli, who is blind. He does all he could to make his dream come true. However, his life takes a new turn when a woman enters his life.

The film also features actors like Padmapriya, Sreya, Arya, TG Ravi, Jagathy Sreekumar, Salim Kumar, Sreehari, Sreekala, Baby Suhaila, Baby Malavika and Master Tejas. Vallabhan and Kaladharan are producing it under the banner of GV Productions and Kamal is directing it. Mohan Sithara has composed the music with lyrics written by Vayalar Sharath Chandra Verma. P Sukumar is the cinematographer, K Rajagopal is the editor and Suh Kollam is the art director. The shooting of the film is currently progressing at a fast pace.
