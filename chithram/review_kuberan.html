Kuberan

Storyline

'Kuberan' tells the story of Siddharthan (Dilip), a young man who lives in a small house that is situated opposite a palatial bungalow. Siddharthan works in a tourist resort nearby. His job there, is to put on the garb of historical and mythological characters like Shivaji, Charlie Chaplin, Hanuman etc and stand absolutely motionless like a statue, to attract the attention of tourists. Visitors then get a chance to try to make him smile or move and those who win are promised a reward. Siddharthan has with him three kids living under his care. He is also in love with Gauri (Uma Shankari), the daughter of Gopanna (Mohan). On the first of every month, Siddharthan and the three kids go to live in the palatial bungalow opposite their small house. There, they enjoy the lifestyle of the rich for one full day. They accomplish this by bribing the caretakers of the house - Theyyunni (Harisri Ashokan) and Abdu (Indrans). But one day they are caught red-handed by Pooja (Samyukta Varma), the real owner of the house. Then follow some very queer and interesting incidents, which make for the climax of 'Kuberan'.

Cast
Dilip, Kalabhavan Mani, Jagathy Sreekumar, Harisri Ashokan, Samyukta Varma
