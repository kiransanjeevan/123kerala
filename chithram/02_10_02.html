Sherlyn Chopra joins Bigg Boss

 

Pin-up girl Sherlyn Chopra has said a definite yes to the Bigg Boss, which begins on October 4 on channel Colors. After Rakhi Sawant and Payal Rohatgi, it is Sherlyn who will provide the oomph appeal in this season's Bigg Boss.

 
 





 
 
Says a source from the channel Colors, "One reason why we were interested in getting Baba Ramdeo on board is because we got Sherlyn. So we wanted to create a Vishwamitra-Menaka kind of situation between the saint and the seductress in the Bigg Boss house . Unfortunately Baba opted out." But the seductress is on.
