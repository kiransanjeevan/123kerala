Jagathy: Having the last laugh

Superstars rise and fade away in quick succession. But, Jagathy Sreekumar reigns supreme in the world of Malayalam comedies. There may be debate over whether Dileep is the numero uno in the industry or not, but such debates prove to be immaterial when it comes to Jagathy. He continues to be the raja of racy humour. Versatility is the hallmark of his brand of humour.

Any serious comedy film-maker in Malayalam cannot afford to miss him if he wants to make a mark. Though maligned with a rape case against him, Jagathy has around 20 films in his hand in Malayalam. The case has been hanging over his head for the last few years. But he proves to be the comedian ultimate, laughing it off.

Though he does not want to discuss the issue, he is taking the court case in his stride. But he is taking his role as a comedian very seriously. ''I love my films. I enjoy the essential thrill of acting,'' he says.

The best thing about Jagathy's acting is that he is absolutely natural in whatever roles he does. ''It is all about observation and then giving expression to them,'' he says.

For a man who came to filmdom with the idea of striking it rich as a hero, Jagathy has not done a bad job as a comedian. In between, he gets to plays serious character roles. He infuses in them his essential humour. And that is the secret of his success.

Jagathy is not so articulate in explaining his craft, which is understandable. He lets his characters speak for themselves. And always makes sure he has the last laugh.
