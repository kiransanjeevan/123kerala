Review: Sadananthante Samayam

Is Dileep and his directors running out of ideas? Sadananthante Samayam is another mandatory comedy that is so muddled and familiar that even Dileep’s Samayam cannot save it. Akbar-Jose who had earlier made Mazhathullikilukkam with Dileep which was a rehash of old Mohanlal hits, has this time ripped from the basic story of Lohithadas’ Bhoothakannadi, and the film has nothing new in presentation. The story is set against the backdrop of a village set in and around Shornur-Ottapalam belt seen in hundreds of films. The protagonist Manakkapally Sadanandan is a man ruled by superstitions and astrology. He believes from childhood that his parents died due to some ‘doshams’ as he was born at a wrong time. All these moulds him into a highly superstitious man and he looks at the ‘rahu kalam’ for stepping out of the house and breaks a coconut everyday in front of the school where he is a teacher! He gets married to Sumangala (Kavya Madhavan) and has a daughter. Due to his weird nature, he loses his job and is forced to leave his ancestral home. He meets a fake astrologer (Jagathy Sreekumar) who makes him believe that he would die on a particular day. Sadanathan becomes a nervous wreck as he prepares for his death!! But will fate prevail over astrology and beliefs…this has to be seen in the climax. Dileep has to do something drastically to change his image of stereotyped characters. He is getting overexposed and a bit stale. Can someone tell Kavya Madhavan to check on her weight, as she looks bloated? The usual battalion of Dileep’s favourite comedians like Salim Kumar, Jagathy and Co are good. On the whole unless Dileep pulls up his socks, his comedy act will turn out to be a great tragedy.
