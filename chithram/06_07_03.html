2007's Top Malayalam hits so far

 

Big B: A technically brilliant film, and that's all. The film did only average business at the box office. Cinematographer-turned-director Amal Neerad's film with Mammootty as the hero seemed influenced by the 2005 Hollywood movie Four Brothers.

The sad part about the whole episode was that besides the technical aspects, the film had no script to bank on. The projected action mood turned women audiences away from the theatres as well.

Kaiyoppu: Though much, much better than crassly commercial ventures, writer-director Ranjith's low budget Kaiyoppu, could have been a better film. The film narrated the life of a reluctant writer who has completed just half of his debut novel.

One of best things about the film was Khushboo, playing a woman who is all set to rekindle her long lost romance with the hero, after a broken marriage to a businessman. The movie had a certain amount of freshness, but with a wafer thin storyline, director Anwar Rasheed could not repeat the success story of Rajamanikyam with this second film.
