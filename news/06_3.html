CPM worker killed, Pannur tense

KANNUR: A CPM worker was hacked to death by an eight-member gang at Pannur on Tuesday, police said. Miscreants reportedly dragged 22-year-old Asharaff, an autorickshaw driver, out of a shop near the busy Pannur busstand and stabbed him several times, police said.

Asharaff died on way to hospital, and the gang fled the seen before police could arrive. A tense situation was prevailing in the area following the incident. Bus traffic came to a standstill and shops downed their shutters even as a heavy posse of police personnel was deployed.
