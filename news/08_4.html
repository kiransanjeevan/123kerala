Panur peaceful, police maintain vigil

KANNUR: The situation in Panur, where a CPM activist was hacked to death on Tuesday by suspected RSS workers, was "peaceful," as police continued to maintain a strict vigil in the area.

According to police, besides maintaining round-the-clock patrolling, search operations were continuing on suspected hideouts to catch the accused responsible for the incident. No untoward incident had been reported in the area, where a dawn-to-dusk hartal was called by the CPM on Wednesday to protest the killing.
