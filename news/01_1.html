Gold biscuit thief in police net

Kochi: An officer of a Coimbatore-based security company, who allegedly stole 10 gold biscuits entrusted by a foreign bank in the company locker, has been arrested by police here.

According to a police press release, the accused, an employee of Group Four Securities, had allegedly removed the biscuits from the company locker at Coimbatore a couple of days ago and left the place along with his paramour for Kochi.

The Switzerland-based Bank of Nova Scotia had entrusted the security company with 5,000 gold biscuits, out of which 10, worth Rs 5 lakh, had been removed, police said. Following a complaint by the Coimbatore police, Kochi Police Commissioner Y Anil Kumar launched a probe.

The Crime Branch Police arrested the accused on March 22 from here and handed him over to Coimbatore police. The accused had already sold five gold biscuits and had bought property worth over Rs 2 lakh at Kasargod.
