I am doing what EMS and Achutha Menon had done, says Antony

THIRUVANANTHAPURAM: Chief Minister A K Antony, under fire from the Opposition and service organisations for invoking ‘‘draconian’’ laws like ESMA to tackle the employees agitation, today pointed out various instances to support his argument that he was only doing what Chief Ministers like E M S Namboodiripad and C Achutha Menon had done.

Antony, keen to put the record straight and obviously having done plenty of ‘‘research’’ on the matter, held that he had not, in fact, acted as harshly as Namboodiripad had one more than once while tackling similar agitations. ‘‘When the employees went on strike in 1969, EMS, who was Chief Minister, said that he would ask the public to tackle the agitation. The NGO Union did not protest and even passed a resolution that it was the responsibility of the employees to support the Government. The police went berserk as primary teachers took out a march to the Secretariat to mark the end of a vehicle jatha from Kasargod. Namboodiripad then said that the lathi in the hands of the police was not to play the flute but to beat people.’’

Citing previous instances when ESMA and similar laws like DIR were used by the CPM and CPI while in power, Antony said that EMS had faced the strike by engineering staff in 1967 by applying DIR and putting them in jail. ‘‘P K Tresia, who was superintending engineer, was arrested from her house at night and put in jail. Alexander, another superintending engineer, was also arrested from his house at 2 am. Krishnankutty, executive engineer, was arrested and put in a cell that housed those convicted for murder. When Secretariat staff went on strike in 1969, EMS invoked the break of service clause to defeat the agitation.’’

When C Achutha Menon was Chief Minister, he used DIR and ESMA to deal with the Government employees and teachers strike in 1971, Antony said. Achutha Menon had also applied dies non (no work, no pay) for the first time in the State to tackle the strike by Government employees in 1973. As many as 110 employees who participated in the strike were dismissed. When KSEB staff went on strike in 1973, then Power Minister M N Govindan Nair has used ESMA and DIR to tackle the agitation. Leaders of various service organisations were arrested and sent to jail in 1975 when ESMA and DIR were again invoked, he added. ‘‘When E K Nayanar was CM during 1987-91, he did not even invite the striking employees for talks. When doctors went on strike in 1994, ESMA was again brought into play.

When the previous Government headed by Nayanar cut down commutation from 50 per cent to 33.25 per cent, many of those who are now spearheading the agitation did not even grumble. There was not even a murmur of protest when a treasury ban was imposed. Plus two teachers did not get their salaries for more than two years but no one complained,’’ Antony said. As a parting shot, he said, ‘‘It was the CPM leadership which showed the way for others by dismissing certain employees from Kairali citing a financial crisis.’’
