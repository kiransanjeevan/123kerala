MVR vs. CPM fight in TVM west

Trivandrum West constituency which is generally not so politically important is witnessing a fight of rare intensity this time. Constituency grabbed headlines when the CMP leader and archrival of CPM M V Raghavan decided to lock horns with the sitting LDF MLA Antony Raju (Kerala Congress (J)). CPM is bent on decimating MVR and that is what gives the fight a rare flavour. BJP which has a sizeable presence in the constituency is making moves keenly. Though it has not fielded any candidate this time there are unconfirmed reports of a secret understanding between CPM and BJP that if BJP does not support MVR here LDF will allow the party to open its account elsewhere in the state. However both the parties are immediate to issue a denial of any such understanding when the news first came on a private television channel.
