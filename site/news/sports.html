A bicentenary match in Tellicherry

Kannur (Kerala), Dec 15: Some of the country’s prominent cricketers, including those of yesteryear, will be seen in action at the municipal stadium grounds in Thalassery here which gears up to celebrate cricket’s bicentenary in March.

“Thalassery, one of the breeding grounds of cricket in the country, is arranging the game’s bicentenary celebrations in a grand manner and former and present Test players will be seen in action here,” P V Sirajuddin, secretary, Kannur District Cricket Association, told.

Players Prasanna, Venkataraghavan, Srinath, Kumble and Dravid were being invited for an exhibition match, he said, adding a Sri Lankan team, comprising all-time greats like Arjuna Ranatunga and Aravinda De Silva, was also likely to visit here. The association, with the support of the state government, was also vying for live coverage of the celebrations by Doordarshan to attract giant sponsors to make the event a memorable one, he said.

Sirajuddin said a huge sum would be involved to host the programme and the state sports and revenue ministers had assured help. “To bring in a foreign team, it involves very high expenditure and we are hopeful that the DD coverage will get us a good amount of sponsorship,” he said. Sri Lankan players like Ranatunga had been already approached to play a match against a south Indian team to be led by Test discard Robin Singh, he said, adding the team would also include former and present Test players who had played here at least once.

“Thalassery being one of the few places where Indian cricket has its origin, we planned to celebrate its bicentenary here,” he said when asked what prompted the association to organise such a grand affair in a town less familiar for the game. “It will also serve as a tribute to those local cricketers who groomed the sports during the past two centuries,” he said, adding a cricket match was played here as early as in 1890.
