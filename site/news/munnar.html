Munnar

Introduction

Munnar is a hill station situated in the eastern part of Kerala, (around 130 kms east of Kochi). It lies in the centre of Kerala's principle tea growing region and can be reached by bus from Kochi, Kottayam, Madurai, Kumily, Coimbatore, Periyar and Kodaikanal.

A favourite summer resort of the British during their reign in India, the tea town of Munnar still retains the quaint old-world charm of the bygone era. Surrounded on all sides by the green, rolling expanse of almost 30 tea gardens, today it is the picturesque, buzzing hub of the tea trade in Kerala.

Munnar is situated at the confluence of three mountain steams, the Mudrapuzha, Nallathanni and Kundala (Munnar literally means "three rivers" in Tamil!). The region's cool climate (maximum temperature 20 degrees centigrade!) is due to its proximity to Anaimudi, which at 2700 metres is the highest peak south of the Himalayas.

Apart from the green carpet of the tea gardens, not many people are aware that part of the Munnar hills are also covered by the rare Neelakurunji plant, which is said to bloom into full profusion once in every 12 years. The next flowering period is slated for the year 2006!

What to do

Munnar offers a host of activity options to appeal to every type. If you are a nature lover then you can relax in the lap of scenic splendour, take long invigorating walks through the tea estates, bathe under sparkling waterfalls or go fishing in the Devikulam Lake.

If you are the adventurous type then you can choose from paragliding, bicycle trips, camping, nature walks, tea factory visits, wildlife treks etc.

Munnar also serves up the assorted culinary delights of South Indian food and don't be surprised if you find yourself reaching out for that freshly brewn, piping hot cup of Munnar tea at the end of a long day!

Access

By Road:  Munnar Bus Stand
Munnar is accessible by road from most of the towns in Kerala. You can also get there from from Madurai (162 kms) and Coimbatore (roughly 160 kms), which are located in the state of Tamil Nadu.

By Rail:  Kochi Railway Station
The nearest railway station is also Kochi. Regular buses and taxis are readily available for Munnar. Some other close railheads are Kottayam (147 km) and Madurai (162 km).

By Air:  Kochi Airport
Kochi is the nearest airport (130 kms away). From Kochi regular buses and taxis are available for the trip up to Munnar.

Best Season

October to May
Apart from the monsoon months (between June and September) Munnar can be visited the whole year through. It would be a good idea to pack a couple of heavy-duty woollens if you are visiting Munnar in the winter months. In summer, however, a light sweater will suffice to keep away the chill.
