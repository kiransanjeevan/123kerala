New impetus to Left unification drive

THIRUVANANTHAPURAM: The CPI proposal to launch a campaign for "communist unification and greater Left unity on a principled basis" in the draft political resolution had got enthusiastic response at the party's 18th congress in session here, party general secretary A B Bardhan said on Saturday.

There was also a common view in the party over the need for evolving an "alternative" to BJP and Congress, Bardhan told reporters. The ideas emerging from the four-day discussions were now before three commissions which would look into at the proposals and the amendments before giving final shape to the party's political line for the next three years.

There had been a "misconception" that CPI viewed the people's front as a failure as it got confined to certain states. What had happened was that the Uttar Pradesh polls followed immediately after the front's formation. "We in CPI are as much committed to an alternative as are other Left and secular parties outside the BJP and the Congress fold." On the lukewarm response from the CPM on the question of communist unification, he said though CPI felt that communist unity was need of the hour, that did not mean that unity parleys would take place immediately.

The thrust of the unity campaign, addressed to communist masses, would be how to reach an agreement on programmes, policies and running a party organisation, he added.
