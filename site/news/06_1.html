Privatisation will effect Kerala: CITU

Kochi: The Centre’s move to privatise and dismantle public sector units in Kerala would seriously affect the economy of the state, Dr M K Pandhe, All-India CITU general secretary, has said.

Addressing a meet-the-press programme organised by the Ernakulam Press Club today, he said the privatisation drive for Cochin Shipyard, FACT, Hindustan Organic Chemicals, HMT, Instrumental India and Cochin Port would further aggravate the economic crisis in Kerala which was already suffering due to cut in import duty and withdrawal of quantitative restrictions on imports.

The support given by the United Democratic Front (UDF) government in the state to the policy of liberalisation was resulting in failure in taking a firm step by the state government against the privatisation drive, he alleged, and appealed to all the political parties in Kerala to pass a resolution in the Assembly opposing the privatisation and closure of public sector units in Kerala.

Pandhe said the committee of public sector union was meeting in New Delhi on March 5 to oppose the policy of privatisation and closure of public sector units. A dharna would be organised before Parliament to show their resentment towards the anti-national policies of the Centre, he said. Pandhe expressed CITU’s solidarity with the six lakh state government employees and teachers who had called for an indefinite strike from February 6.

Appreciating the decision of the state government to hold talks with the leaders, he condemned the promulgation of ESMA banning strike in some services. Such measures only hamper smooth and meaningful negotiation, he added.
