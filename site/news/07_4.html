Strike paralyses offices in Kerala

Thiruvananthapuram: The functioning of government offices and educational institutions in Kerala virtually came to a grinding halt today as nearly five lakh employees and teachers began an indefinite strike since midnight last night against the United Democratic Front government's cost-cutting measures.

According to reports received from across the state, the strike was total in all departments. However, essential services like transport, electricity and water and milk supply have been spared. Police said no violence was reported from anywhere in the state.

The ministerial staff in the Kochi High Court are, however, staying out of the strike. Service and teachers' organisations, setting aside political loyalties, are participating in the strike after yesterday's talks between the unions and the government failed. The employees' demands include restoration of benefits like leave surrender facility, which had been withdrawn by the A K Antony government in view of the state's precarious financial position. Security has been stepped up all over the state to face the strike, one of the biggest agitations in recent years in which pro-Left service and teachers outfits as well as pro-UDF organisations are participating.

The striking employees also took out a march here. The state cabinet is meeting this afternoon to take stock of the situation. Around 200 employees, mostly top civil servants and staff in ministers' offices, turned up for work in the government Secretariat here amidst tight security. Chief Minister A K Antony and his colleagues are attending office, a CMO official said. Only one of the three main entrances of the Secretariat was opened for the working staff to enter.

A Kochi report said the strike in the government offices in the district was total and Kochi University of Science and Technology and schools and colleges did not function. Employees in the co-operative sector too are on strike. The functioning of the High Court, declared an essential service by the Chief Justice, did not suffer as the High Court Staff Associations are not participating.
