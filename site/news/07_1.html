+2: Govt. given time till March 17

Kochi: A division Bench of the Kerala High Court today granted government time till March 17 to pass final orders regarding the grant of plus two schools throughout the state. Earlier, the Bench, comprising Justice K S Radhakrishnan and Justice K A Mohammad Shafi, had directed government to consider the matter before today.

On expiry of time limit government sought two months time to comply with the directions of the court. The Bench, however, made it clear that no further time would be granted to the government after March 17 for this purpose. The order was passed when 82 writ appeals, filed by the government against orders of a single judge granting and directing to consider grant of plus two to certain schools in the state, came up before the Bench.
