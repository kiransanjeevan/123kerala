Govt to table Narendran Report in Assembly

THIRUVANANTHAPURAM: The Kerala cabinet on Wednesday decided to place in the assembly the report of Narendran commission that went into the question of adequate representation of backward and minorities in government departments, PSUs and other autonomous bodies.

Briefing reporters after the cabinet meeting, Chief Minister A K Antony said the report was dicussed in detail and it was decided to place the same in the house during the next session, beginning in June. The cabinet also decided to give a no objection certificate to the Periyaram medical college to start B Pharm course, he said.

The cabinet also decided to prepare a schedule for giving payment of arrears due to PWD contractors, he said, adding the first instalement of arrears would be paid before Onam. The total arrears to contractors would come to a tune of Rs 700 crore, he added. To a question, he said the reforms committee had suggested a five-day week for governemnt employees. The suggestion had so far not come up before the cabinet.
