Spice Congress on schedule

Kochi, Dec 13: The 'World Spice Congress 2002' would be held here from January 31 to February 2. A statement jointly released by Spices Board chairman C J Jose and All-India Spices Exporters Forum (Kochi) chairman M S A Kumar said delegates from over 21 countries, including the United States and the United Kingdom, would participate in the event.

The World Spice Congress was the sixth in the series focusing on the changing global scenario in the wake of the WTO regime. The programme would showcase the Indian spice industry's capabilities to meet the changing quality requirements of the global trade, it said.

An exhibition to be held at the venue of the congress had also received a positive response and all the stalls have been booked, it added.
