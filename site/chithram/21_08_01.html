Bikini mania comes to Bollywood again with Rani and Lara

 

One can't understand why Bollywood is fixated with bikinis - they publicize any shots of an actress wearing one to no degree while ignoring all the other scantily clad women who just don't happen to be wearing swimwear (and often...very little else!). But after the huge amount of buzz that bikini clad actresses have craetdd in recent years - Esha Deol in Dhoom, Kareena Kapoor in Tashan and Kambakkht Ishq, Amisha Patel in Thoda Pyar Thoda Magic - this season will see two more actresses being talked about for their choice of swimwear. Lara Dutta dons one for the upcoming Akshay Kumar-Sanjay Dutt film Blue (given the amount of underwater stunts, we can't imagine what other choice Lara had) and Rani Mukherjee in Dil Bole Hadippa.

 
 





 
 
Rani is attempting to revive her flagging career after a series of flops with Dil Bole Hadippa - which stars Shahid Kapur and releases this September. Word has it that Yash Raj Films' Aditya Chopra delayed the release of Dil Bole Hadippa so that the date that marks Rani's two years of bad luck would have passed by then. Meanwhile, given the huge success that Kaminey has become, we wonder if Shahid Kapur may be the trick to getting Rani's career back on track…
