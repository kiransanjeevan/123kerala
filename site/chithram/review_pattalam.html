Review: Pattalam

Lal Jose, the director of Pattalam seems to be totally confused on whether to make the film a comedy or an action film! The film crashes under the strain of pulling in different directions and the viewer will not enjoy this roller-coaster ride that is sure to induce a headache.

What on earth prompted Lal Jose to make Pattalam? Is this the same director who gave us great rural comedies like Oru Maravathur Kanavu, Chandranudikkunna Dikkil and Meesa Madhavan? The director operates on the belief that having an army of comedians on screen like Jagathy Sreekumar, Innocent, Salim Kumar, Indrans, Oduvil, Machan Vargese, Mamookoya, Idavela Babu, Zeenat and Bindu Panikkar is enough to make the audience laugh, then he has gone wrong!

The film is set in the background of Ponnarimangalam, a typical sylvian village, where you see characters like a local sub inspector, milkman, small politicians, pump operator, a panchayat president and an ayurveda physician played by the above mentioned comedians. When the film opens we are told that there is a communal tension brewing in this peaceful village which later forms an excuse for the screenplay writer to introduce a unit of Rapid Action Force (RAF) led by Captain Pattabhiraman (Mammootty) and his assistant Benny (Biju menon) as peace keepers.

The villagers are initially hostile to the idea of RAF but soon after a series of comic incidents they start adoring the soldiers. Capt Pattabhiraman, a chronic Bachelor all these years develops a soft corner for Vimala (Tessa), an LIC agent and a war widow. Later in a long drawn-out flashback it is revealed that Pattabhiraman was responsible for the death of Lieutenant Nandu (Indrajeet), Vimala’s husband who had to cancel his leave and go back to the North-East to fight terrorists on the day of his marriage! Meanwhile Benny, captain’s assistant has a good time with Satyabhama (Jyothirmayi) and gets married to her. More trouble in the form of some terrorist organizations, which tries to poison the villager’s water tank.

Hello, where is the screenplay, which is totally going haywire? The RAF was sent as peacekeepers to communally sensitive village. But there are no talks of conflict happening and the attention is diverted to other matters. So where was the crisis in the first place? The debut scriptwriter Reji Nair is unable to tie up the loose ends, so the ending appears contrived. The film simply lacks heart, characterisation and humour of earlier Lal Jose films. All the required elements for an army action drama is unfortunately missing and the few script short cuts gives the movie an amateurish feel.

All the comedians ham it out and Biju Menon as Benny in a comedy role seems quite out of sorts. Jyothirmayi as his romantic interest has proved that she is a good actress while new find Tessa has miles to go as far as emoting goes. Mammootty as Captain Pattabhiraman laces his portrayal with humour and dry wit. His foray into comedy with the backing of Lal Jose maybe well intentioned but audience accepting him in a full-length comedy is still in doubt. The songs tuned by Vidyasagar are average but the picturisation has fallen flat.
