'I am not against glamour': Padmapriya

She made her debut in Malayalam with that off-beat classic Kazhcha as Mammootty’s wife and then did the role of a Muslim girl who falls love with a Hindu in Sibi Malayil directed Amritham opposite Jayaram. She was the heroine in 2005’s biggest blockbuster Rajamanickam. Padmapriya is an MBA in Finance and worked for three years with GE (General Electric) as a risk consultant. Her latest release Vadakkumnathan is also a big hit, in which she plays Meera who falls in love with an eccentric Sanskrit professor played by Mohanlal. Padmapriya in conversation..

Your performance in Vadakkumnathan is getting a lot accolades. Please comment?
(Smiles) Yes, Meera was a well-etched out role that Girish Puthencherry had written. I am happy to be a part of an excellent movie with some of the best actors in Malayalam. They all helped me a lot and I am thankful to the audience of Kerala who have accepted me.

Will you do a glamour role in Malayalam? How comfortable are you to do such roles?
I am not against glamour. I believe in sensuality not vulgarity. One should attract the audience not distract them. I am sure that I will be able to do a glamour role with the same ease that I did Lakshmi in Kazcha or Meera in Vadakkumnathan.

What are your future movies?
In Malayalam I am doing Ashwaroodan with Suresh Gopi directed by Jayaraj which will be a revelation of sorts where I play Seetalakshmi Antharjanam and “Bhagvata Charitram...” with Mammootty and Sreenivasan directed by Jomon which is a fun movie. I am doing a film for Grihalakshmi Productions with Sreenivasan as hero under the direction of V.M.Vinu.

Have you learned Malayalam?
Yes, I do understand Malayalam very well, though I can't speak fluently. But soon you will find me doing that (Smiles).

You have done films with Mammootty and Mohanlal , the superstars of Malayalam? How different are they to work with?
It’s difficult to find stars who are such extraordinary actors. It’s beyond my capacity to comment about them. They are the best in Indian cinema.
