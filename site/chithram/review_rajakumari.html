Review: Nakshatrakannulla Rajakumaran Avanundoru Rajakumari

Prithviraj, who makes his debut on screen as Ananthu (his first film Nandanam is yet to be released), has a charming presence and is appealing. He might very well be the next new star in Malayalam. Gayathri Raghuram as Achchu does a good job. Debutante Anjali Krishna as Raziya Begum brings a touch of freshness. Renju, who too makes his debut, is well cast as Karthik. Lyrics by Rameshan Nair, set to tune by Benny and Kannan pass muster. Ramachandra Babu, the cameraman and Premachandran, the art-director manage to capture the mood of the film. A light hearted family entertainer. Good way to spend an evening.

Storyline

The film revolves around a family that has been claiming descent from the brave 'chekavars'- the warriors of the 'Vadakkanpaattu', the folklore of Kerala. Bhageerathi Amma has two sons Veerabhadra Kurup and Kochchukurup and a daughter Subhadra. Veerabhadra Kurup and Subhadra don't like each other and behave like sworn enemies. It is in this context that Ananthu, the young son of Subhadra and Aswathy alias Achchu, the young daughter of Veerabhadra Kurup, fall in love, sincere, serious love. They get timely assistance from Chandutty, a goldsmith, Bhaskaran, a barber, and Shankunni, a singer of folksongs, in passing messages, meeting secretly to keep their romance alive. They hope that Achchu's brother Karthik, who is in Delhi and who is a friend of Ananthu, will help them too. But when Karthik turns out to be against this alliance, all their hopes vanish. Ananthu and his aides come across Raziya Begum who could be of use to them in this matter. Even Kochchukurup, who is against his siblings in some ways, comes up to help Ananthu and Achchu. What happens afterwards forms the plot of Nakshatrakannulla Rajakumaran...
