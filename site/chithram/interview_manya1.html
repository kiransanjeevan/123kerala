Manya talks about marriage

After acting in a few films in Malayalam, Manya almost disappeared. She is now on a comeback mission. The actress was in a mood to talk about her future plans.

Why did you disappear for almost a year?

My last film in Malayalam was ‘Aparichitan’. Offers kept coming in but I wasn’t able to accept them because I was busy with Telugu and Kannada films. I had acted in all the South Indian languages. I got all my good offers only in Malayalam. before entering Malayalam films, I had acted in Kannada and Telugu, but they never gave priority to acting.

Tell us about your family.

My dad is a doctor in London. That was where I grew up before returning to Hyderabad. My mom is a housewife. I have a younger sister. Right now, I’m busy pursuing my PG degree through correspondence.

Marriage?

I have decided to not get married for atleast another two years. But, mother has already started to look for prospective grooms.

Would you continue acting after marriage?

I don’t want to lie. Once you get into this field, it is very hard to resist the temptation to act. I intend to continue in the film industry either in front of or behind the camera.

Atleast she is honest enough to admit!
