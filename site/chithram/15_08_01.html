Geethu Mohandas, Manoj go to London

KP Kumaran's 'Aakashagopuram' is fast progressing in London. The film stars Mohanlal in the lead role of a sculptor. Shwetha Menon is the heroine.

Geethu Mohandas is playing an important role. So is Manoj K Jayan. Both of them took off to London recently to star in the film.

After a brief lull in her career, Geethu Mohandas is making a comeback of sorts in Malayalam. Manoj and Geethu are starring together in 'Arunan', directed by Vinod Mankara. Manoj K Jayan has almost completed his 'Vaalmikam' with Meera Vasudevan.

'Aakashagopuram' is likely to be released after the Onam season.
