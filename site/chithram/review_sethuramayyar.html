Sethuramayyar CBI

Sethuramayyar CBI is the third part of Oru CBI Dairy Kurippu which was a super hit 16 years back. Even today the audience have not forgotten Mammootty's role as an intelligent CBI officer who had solved many cases with his brains. The newly dusted CBI story is successful to a very large extent, with Mammootty's immense screen presence and performance.

This time Sethuramayyar (Mammootty) the CBI officer is on Onam holidays to Kerala to spend a few days with his sister and nieces Rashmi (Navya) and Rachana (Nandana). A local parish priest meets Sethuramayyar and requests him to meet Alex (Kalabhavan Mani) a convict sentenced to death for six murders. He meets him at the jail and Alex confesses that he has killed only five people and he is innocent about the death of the sixth man, a financier.

He convinces the CBI officer that someone else has done the crime and has put the blame on him. Sethuramayyar gets interested in the case and along with his team of Chacko (Mukesh), Vikram (Jagathy) and Ganesh (Vineeth Kumar) starts investigating the case. His nieces also help him by using common sense, at the right time. How the CBI officer and his team finally reach the culprit who committed the sixth murder forms the rest of the investigative story.

The film is produced Liberty Basheer and C Vijayan Menon. Mammootty invests so much conviction and energy to the character of Sethuramayyar that it is a treat to watch him. We cannot think about another actor who can do this role with ease like him. Rest of the characters has all done their roles well. The romantic scenes between Vineeth and Navya spoil the tempo at times, but on the whole the film is interesting and is worth a look.
