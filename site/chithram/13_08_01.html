'I am as good as ever': Suresh Gopi

Suresh Gopi has made a great comeback with Bharathchandran IPS. The USP of Suresh Gopi is his immense screen presence and “macho action hero” image, an actor who could deliver his dialogues with conviction. `Ormayundo Ee Mukham?`- Remember that famous dialogue uttered by Suresh Gopi in Commissioner (1994), which became a popular line among Malayalees? In this exclusive interview two days after the release of the film, Gopi opens up.

Bharathchandran IPS has taken a super opening at the Kerala box-office. Please comment Yes.
The film has taken the best ever opening in recent times. Theatres are overflowing and on the second day itself, there is repeat audience. Now I can breathe more easily as there was a canard spread by some of my so-called-friends that I am a discarded actor. This opening has proved that I am as good as ever (smiles).

So Bharathchandran IPS is the perfect character to make a comeback as an action hero…
The audiences are fed up of our politicians and bureaucracy. They think that Bharathchandran had the magic potion to solve all their problems as he is the man who gets things done with scant regard for the rulers of the day. The last time I was around in Commissioner, people appreciated me and now, after11 years, our society is seeped in corruption and the credibility of our politicians are all at an all time low. So we need a Bharathchandran IPS more than ever before.

Tell us more about Bharathchandran who has now become a super cop!
(Smiles) Bharathchandran is a character created by writer Ranji Panikkar and he is an honest hard working cop who bends rules and sometimes breaks them in the pursuit of the criminal element. He has no regard for the constitutional rights of even suspects, which gives him a mass appeal. Bharathchandran had struck a chord with the audience of Kerala as he speaks the language of the common man peppered with day-to-day usages.

Why did you take such a long break before doing an action film?
I did not want to popularize the gun culture as it created a negative impression among the next generation. I took a brief sabbatical from action films after Nariman(2001). But all the people whom I talked to, like the common man on the streets to college students especially girls wanted me to take up the gun and clean up the system of corruption and rampant communalism prevailing in our society today.

We believe that you have signed five films…
Yes.. I have decided to be a part of mainstream commercial cinema. I am here to take my share of the box-office. I have signed with top banners and directors like Shaji Kailas, K.Madhu and Anil Menon. If my audiences want me, I am sure to provide them with maximum entertainment.
