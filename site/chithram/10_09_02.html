Jayaraj makes an actor of yet another director

 

It was ten years back, in 1997 that Jayaraj first attempted to make an actor out of a very successful director. Lal, then known as Lal of the Siddique-Lal duo, was introduced as an actor by Jayaraj in his ‘Kaliyattom’, an adaptation of Othello. After acting in the movie, Lal, who had done the character of Paniyan (Iago in ‘Othello’), even declared that acting was not his cup of tea and that he would never face the camera again. But that was not to be. Lal got noted as an actor and he went on acting, in films after films. And now this busy producer-distributor (he had not directed a movie for years now) is a busy actor too.

And now, in 2008, Jayaraj is all set to repeat the feat again, with ‘Gulmohar’. And the director whom he is introducing this time as an actor is none other than scenarist-turned-director Ranjith, who is also a friend to Jayaraj.

This is not the first time that Ranjith is acting. A School of Drama alumnus that he is, Ranjith had acted in some plays and had faced the camera once in ‘Oru May Maasa Pulariyil’(1987) in a small role (The film was scripted by Ranjith himself and was directed by V.R.Gopinath).

 
 





 
 
‘Gulmohar’, set in the 1980’s, was initially planned with Suresh Gopi in the lead role. But when, due to certain reasons, the actor couldn’t do the film, Jayaraj had to think of another option and it was then that he asked Ranjith to do the role. Though Ranjith was not confident about it, Jayaraj’s confidence and his insistence that Ranjith should do the role made him take up the acting assignment.

Ranjith plays in ‘Gulmohar’ a character named Induchoodan, a youthful college lecturer who shares the revolutionary fervour of the times. Scripted by Didi Damodaran, the daughter of noted scenarist T.Damodaran, ‘Gulmohar’ has in the cast Siddique, Neenu Mathew, Meghanathan, Nishanth Sagar, Manoj.K.Jayan, I.M.Vijayan etc and features music by Johnson.
