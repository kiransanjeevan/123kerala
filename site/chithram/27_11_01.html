Gopika signs for a Telugu film

Gopika has achieved a seemingly impossible feat! This is one actress who doesn’t believe in getting glamorous and still has offers coming her way.

Gopika is currently doing a film with Dileep in the lead, the success of ‘Chaandupottu’ seems to have had a great influence on this selection. And she also has new offers from Telugu films, an industry known for its penchant for glamour. She is doing the female lead in an action-thriller. Gopika is playing the role of an investigative journalist in the film.

Her last film in Tamil with Simbu, ‘Thotti Jaya’ turned out to be a dud at the box office, but she has established herself as an actress with caliber.
