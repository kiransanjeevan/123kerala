Kavya’s offbeat ways

 

After dabbling generously and quite successfully with the commercial potboilers, Kavya Madhavan is now struggling to find a footing with offhand flicks. Earlier Kavya proved her mettle in the role of Subhadra in ‘Naalu Pennungal’. And now she is all set to impress again by donning Vaikam Muhammed Basheer’s famous creation ‘Kunji Pathumma’ on screen. The film titled ‘Ente Uppappakkoraana Undayirunnu’ will be produced by Radiant Group and will be directed by KP Kumaran.

 
 





 
 
For now K P Kumaran is busy winding up with Mohanlal starrer ‘Akashagopuram’ inspired from Ibsen’s ‘The Master Builder’. Although an offbeat flick, this one boasts of a great many big names counting Manoj K Jayan, Shwetha Menon, Geethu Mohandas and Nithya. The film is all set to hit the marquee by the second week of March.
