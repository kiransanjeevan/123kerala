The man behind Mayavi

 

For director Shafi, churning out hits by now has become almost a habit. His latest offering, Mayavi with Mammootty in the title role has set the cash registers ringing in a big way. Instead of resting on his laurels, however the man is already busy with his new venture, which have youngsters Prithviraj and Jayasurya in the lead.

The director of such films as One Man Show, Kalyana Raman, Pulival Kalyanam, Thommanum Makkalum and Maja finds narrating serious subjects in a lighter vein effective. This is one of the reasons why Roberto Benigni's Life is Beautiful is one of his favourite films.

In an exclusive interview, the director talks about his films, career and dreams.

You should be thrilled by the fabulous response to Mayavi?
Absolutely. The film was well received by the audience.

What do you think worked for the film?
I think it was the screenplay by Rafi-Mecartin. A well-written script can make things easy for the director. We started shooting for the film with a completed script and it helped the whole proceedings to a great extent.

Mammootty has proven that he has the knack to tickle the funny bone. What is special about his humour in Mayavi?
The subtle humour of the character is the film's highlight. I feel that situational comedy appeals more with the audience while narrating certain themes like this one. Let us not forget that Mammootty has a flair for comedy and this is evident from films like Oru Maravathoor Kanavu and Kottayam Kunjachan. In Mayavi, we have used humour to narrate a genuine story.

You have directed Vikram in Maja. How was the experience of doing a Tamil film?
Believe me, it was like a picnic and I have thoroughly enjoyed making the film.

On looking back, are you happy the way your career has shaped up?
(Smiles) I believe I have enough reason to feel happy. I do feel that my first three films belonged to a certain genre. Mayavi may have been treated in a similar way as Thommanum Makkalum, but still I regard it as my best film yet.

You haven't done a film with Mohanlal as yet?
That is a dream. I am in the search for a script that suits him and hope to work with him soon.

Your next film is with Prithviraj and Jayasurya. What is the film about?
It's a campus love story and has been set quite differently from all my earlier films. I am in the process of completing the script with the scenarist duo of Sachi and Sethu. The shooting will begin this April.
