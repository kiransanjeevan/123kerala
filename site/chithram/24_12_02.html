Asin says good bye to Tamil films

 

Finally, actress Asin has decided to say good bye to Tamil film industry, the tinsel world, which made her as a professional and join as a full timer in Bollywood!! The latest buzz reveals that the actress has signed up a one-year contract with a leading Mumbai based company. The company is doing celebrity management and got her entire dates for the whole year for a whopping Rs 8-10 crores.

Hereafter, the actress should act according to the schedule fixed by the company. As per the contract, the company will manage her dates for films and commercials and no one can approach the actress directly. She will have to earmark certain number of days for the seven endorsements that she is doing at the moment plus two international endorsements that she has signed last week. In addition, the company will also manage her dates and negotiate with her producers.

 
 





 
 
Asin's first Hindi movie will be hit the screens shortly. There are three big companies seeking her call sheets but the actress asked them to wait for the release of Ghajini. She also advised her new celebrity management company to give prime importance for those three production companies. The company also denied dates for three big production houses in Tamil including Rajkamal owned by none other than our Ulaga Nayagan Kamal Hassan!
