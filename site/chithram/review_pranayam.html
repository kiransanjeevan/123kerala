Pranayamanithooval

Storyline
Pranayamanithooval is the story of a young Meera, who follows around Balu, a model. Balu comes to realize that Meera is following and watching him. He thinks that she is in love with him and decides to ask her for a photograph, on being told to do so by his friends Ponnappan and Sundaran. But before Balu gets a chance to ask her for a photo, she asks him to give her his photograph. He is only too happy to do so. Balu and his friends are sure that Meera is in love with her. Balu's mother comes to meet Meera, who stays in a hostel just beside Balu's house. It is only with Balu's mother arrival that Meera gets an inkling of Balu's love for her. But she has her own different love story, with a young man, Vinu. This causes certain twists in the story, for Vinu died in an accident. And there is something that links Balu to Vinu in a very strange way.

Review
Vineeth Kumar is OK as Balu, but he does not look as attractive and charming as he looked in Devadootan and Kanmashi. Debutante Gopika, who plays Meera, too is OK, though she lacks the freshness of a debutante. Jayasurya has not got much to do as Vinu. The main flaw with the film lies with the script by Kaloor Dennis.
