The big hope!

Madhu Warrier the younger brother of Manju Warrier wife of Dileep has something to cheer about. He is walking with a big smile on his face after Bharathchandran IPS has been declared a hit. His role as Anwar, a police officer in the film has given him a new lease of life and next week his debut film Campus which was lying in the cans for long is finally releasing. Madhu had also done a few forgettable films in between which were all flops.

Now Madhu is pinning all hope on Campus directed by veteran Mohan that carries good reports.
