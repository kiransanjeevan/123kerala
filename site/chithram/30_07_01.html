Nadia's plans for her daughter

Nadia Moidu did make an impressive comeback with the Tamil film 'M Kumaran s/o Mahalakshmi', but for some strange reasons, she wasn't able to capitalize on the fabulous re-launch.

Now, nobody knows what she's up to. But, some media in Tamil have started to circulate stories that Nadia is seriously planning to launch her daughter in films. She has a daughter who's studying in Class IX and the media report that she is planning to bring her daughter into films after a few years. Nadia hasn't confirmed anything.

As far as Nadia's career is concerned, she did get plenty of offers after the re-entry films but nothing much happened because she was looking for heroine-oriented roles. According to rumors, she was also demanding huge salaries.

Radha, former heroine of yesteryears, too is getting ready to launch her daughter in films. The daughter too is in Class IX.
