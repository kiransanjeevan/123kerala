Titbits

 

Delicate beauty dares to get bold: She has been known as the girl next door for most of her movies and even her roles were etched out in that fashion. But now she has decided to break the path and is all set to take on some glamorous skin show flicks. She is none other than Bhavana and the pretty girl says that this decision is completely her own and no one has compelled her to do this. She will soon be seen in the Mohanlal starrer 'Sagar alias Jackie-Reloaded' and then 'Casanova'.



'Thirakkatha' gets triple treat" The recent Idea-Mathrubhumi-Amrita Film Awards have been announced and as expected, the much talked movie 'Thirakkatha' has lopped up not one or two but a total of three major awards. While Ranjith has bagged the best director, the film itself has been adjudged as the best movie and not stopping at that, the buxom beauty Priyamani has been given the best actress award.

 
 





 
 
More flicks from Tollywood to Mollywood: It appears that since the name of the game is success and revenue, the Malayalam industry is not stopping at anything that can entertain the audience. In this process, it is now being learnt that they have begun to rope in more Telugu movies in dubbed versions since the response to those flicks has been quite good. Now, two new movies 'King' starring Nagarjuna and 'Kotha Bangaru Lokam' starring budding hero Varun Sandesh are in the process of getting dubbed into Malayalam.
