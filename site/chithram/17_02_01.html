Sexy actresses now seduce in sarees

 

Bollywood babes have been showing off their well-shaped curves and sexy legs from their cocktail dresses, skirts and tops but it is only sari our desi wear that makes these girls look super sexy and desirable. Every time these ladies step-out in lovely designer saris, heads are bound to turn on their fashion statements.


Beauty queen Aishwarya
Aishwarya looked every bit of a dignified Indian woman in a vibrant red colored saree, matched with a black shawl and hair bun in the melodious title track of the movie ‘Hum Dil De Chuke Sanam’. She was draped in beautiful blue Banarasi embroidered saree embellished with beads and stones in movie ‘Devdas’, which was a rage among women and these Neeta Lulla creations made her look like a true Indian Goddess.






Glam Doll Kat
Mostly seen in western clothes, when this Brit Babe donned a saree for the very first time, it had to be a Manish Malhotra creation. Glittering in a black saree with red border in the song ‘Teri Ore’ from movie ‘Singh is King’ Kat looked hotter than ever before. Kat swaying in a pink saree made many men’s heart beat faster, as never before had Katrina looked so graceful and lovable.










Vivacious Kareena
No one has forgotten Bebo darling getting wet in rains and happily singing ‘Bhagey re mann’ with sexily draped red floral print saree with balloon sleeved blue blouse on her that made men go weak in their knees. This beautiful saree was Rocky S’s piece of work.




Desi girl Priyanka
Priyanka looked smoking hot in sparkling georgette saree and halter blouse in rocking ‘Desi girl’ song. Her sans-ornament look went very well with a saree, which was later adopted by many stylish young women. She again donned orange and pink shaded saree for melodious ‘Jao na’ and Maharashtrian saree in playful ‘Raat ke Dhaai ‘songs that made her look totally graceful. Every time this desi girl has slipped into a saree she has redefined sensuality.

 
 





 
 




Queen Bee Rani
This brilliant actress dazzled when Sabyasachi Mukherjee dressed her in silk sarees with zardosi and stone work of colorful combinations like dark red and yellow. Stunning kundan trinkets by ‘Tanishq’ matched to the elegent silk sarees gave her an authentic Rajasthani look in the movie ‘Paheli’. Rani made every girl envious of her rich ethnic look in the songs like ‘Dheere Jalna’.





Sexy Sush
Style diva Sushmita Sen donned lovely chiffon sarees in ‘Main Hoon Na’ and this sexy chemistry teacher swept many men off their feet including ‘King Khan’. Manish Malhotra draped the tall beauty in gorgeous peach pale georgette for the song ‘Tumhe Jo Maine Dekha’ and in Black saree with big red polka dots that became a rage among women. This beauty queen made sarees look glamorous.




Enchanting Kajol
Kajol was at her sexiest best when Manish Malhotra made her wear sarees of green, blood red, black, golden, white and sky blue colors in the song ‘Suraj hua Madhham’. She brought back the trend of single colored sarees with minimal or no ornaments to go with it. She enchanted audiences with her Kohl-rimmed eyes to add on her gorgeous sarees.




Sweet Amrita Rao
Amrita Rao was made to wear beautiful dark colored silk sarees and purple shiny saree with diamond necklace in the movie ‘Vivah’ which was perfect for her bride-to-be avatar. She again played village belle in Shyam Benegal’s flick ‘Welcome to Sajjanpur’ in which she draped floral print simple sarees to give her role genuine look and audience have always like this cute actress in the role of a simpleton.
