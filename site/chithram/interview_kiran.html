Interview: Kiran Tandon

The girl from a place that is known for its marble stones, Jaipur.

Tamil cinema, which is obsessed with girls from the North, is ready to worship her this time. She was launched by the prestigious AVM for its film 'Gemini' as Vikram's heroine.

Tamil cinema loves glamour. Kiran is more than willing to display hers. That's why she is 'hot property' amidst producers and directors. Added to this is the fat that all the three films in Tamil that she had done have succeeded commercially. She is a lucky star now. How important do you think luck is? Ask Isha Kopikkar- an actress who had all the good lucks a female would ever need, but she couldn't succeed because her films bombed and she got labeled as an unlucky star. Let's get back to Kiran.

How did a girl from Jaipur royal family end up in movies?

I had gone to my uncle's house in Mumbai after writing my class XII exams. His friend was making the music video of an album called 'Punjabi Munda'. He asked me if I was interested in appearing in it. My uncle encouraged me to accept it. I did. That was how I got introduced to Raj Kumar Santoshi. That led me to the Coca Cola commercial.

I then got an offer to work for Subhash Gai's 'Yaadein', in which I played Hritik's heroine. That film changed my life. Soon, 'Gemini' happened. Director Charan was looking for a girl to play the role of a Marwari girl in the film.

You did a film with Mohanlal too, didn't you?

Shaji Kailash offered me the role in 'Taandavam' only after he saw me in 'Gemini'. I believe that it was luck that had got me the role.

What do you feel about Mohanlal?

He is a genius. I would love to do more Malayalam films.

What was it like to act with Kamal?

'Anbae Shivam' gave me the opportunity to act with him. He is a gentleman actor.

There is an accusation that you are too glamorous…

People who make films are putting in lot of money. If the character and the story demand some glamour, I don't mind. I wouldn't agree to show unnecessary glamour if the scene or character doesn't require it.

Will you shift residence from Jaipur to Chennai?

I'm planning to buy a house in Chennai. I have only a few films right now. If offers increase, I shall give serious thought to it. .
