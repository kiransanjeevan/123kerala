Rosshan Andrrews to make Nadodikattu sequel

 

Rosshan Andrrews of Udayanaanu Thaaram fame is to direct the fourth part of Nadodikattu, featuring Mohanlal and Sreenivasan as Dasan and Vijayan, the funny CID officers of Kerala Police. It was in 1987 that Nadodikattu was made, with the fictitious duo of Dasan and Vijayan, making their first appearance in Malayalam Cinema. The film was a success and Nadodikattu got a sequel the next year in Pattanapravesham, both the films being scripted by Sreenivasan and directed by Satyan Anthicaud.

 
 





 
 
And then in 1990 came the third part, Akkare Akkare Akkare, in which Dasan and Vijayan were cracking cases in their own style in the U.S. This third film in the series was directed by Priyadarshan, with script of course by Sreenivasan. And now, most likely early in 2009, Dasan and Vijayan will make their appearance once again, in a fourth part of the series, to be directed by Rosshan Andrrews. The fourth part, titled Dasanum Vijayanum will anyhow have script by James Albert of Classmates fame, who is presently busy with his Cycle, which is in the shooting stage. Dasanum Vijayanum will most likely take off by the end of 2008 and may get released in 2009.
