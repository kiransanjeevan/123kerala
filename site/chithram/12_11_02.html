Blessy to direct Jenny's film

 

When Meera Jasmine's family was going through acute financial troubles, she got into films. It was a survival decision. But the family has recovered so fabulously now that her elder sister Jenny Jasmine has decided to produce a film. About two years ago, an ugly public fight broke out in the Jasmine household regarding the way her family managed her earnings. Though they claim that they have settled the dispute, Meera Jasmine had almost started managing her finances independently.

 
 





 
 
At around the same time, her elder sister Jenny Jasmine decided to make her debut in films. She acted as a character artiste in a film or two. That was about it. But now she is going to produce her own film (where did the money come from???). Blessy, the man who introduced Meera to Lohitadas after seeing her sing at his church's choir, is going to direct this film. Jenny met the director on the sets of his new film 'Kolkotta News' and gave him the advance. Ironically, Meera Jasmine is the heroine of his current film.
