Top Malayalam Actresses of 2008

 

This year did not see many female-oriented Malayalam movies, unlike the 1960s, 1970s and even 1980s.

Things have changed quite a bit in cinema since then, and the current crop of actresses only add to the film's glamour quotient. Yet, we have had some notable female roles this year. We take a look:

Priyamani
Film: Thirakkadha

National award winner Priyamani came up with a sterling performance in Ranjith's Thirakkadha. Her character, Malavika, has been modelled on the life of yesteryear actress Sreevidya. She fell in love and married her co-star Ajayachandran. But soon, differences cropped up in their relationship.

When a young director Akbar Ahmed went in search of her, she was suffering from cancer and living alone in a modest hospital room.

Gopika
Film: Veruthe Oru Bharya

Gopika bid farewell to films after marriage, but not before giving a strong performance in Akku Akbar's Veruthe Oru Bharya.

The film revolved around an exhausted housewife, who was not treated well by her husband (played by Jayaram). One day, she decides to call it quits. Her character was named Bindu, which meant a dot. According the director, the name itself suggested that her life was not going anywhere.

Months after its release, Veruthe Oru Bharya is still running to packed houses.

Sukumari
Film: Mizhikal Sakshi

Director Asok R Nath's Mizhikal Sakshi witnessed a top notch performance by veteran actor Sukumari. The film dealt with old age-related issues, after a mute old lady -- fondly called Kuniyamma -- starts living in a temple. When it was learnt that she is, in fact, a Muslim, the incident leads to tension between the communities. Later, it is revealed that the fundamentalists had framed her son, a lecturer, and sent him to the gallows. Her son was played by Mohanlal.

 
 





 
 
Jyothirmayee
Film: Adayalangal

Despite a brief role in the award winning film, Jyothirmayee made a strong impact.
In Adayalangal, the beautiful Meenakshikkutty becomes the only solace in the tragic life of the film's protagonist, Gopi. The role was quite challenging, but Jyothirmayee played it out naturally.

Meera Jasmine
Film: Innathe Chinthavishayam

Meera Jasmine did not have a great 2008, as none of her films did well at the box office. Yet, she was the only heroine to make an impact at the initial collections.

She started the year with Blessy's Calcutta News with Dileep, followed by Sathyan Anthikkad's Innathe Chinthavishayam, in which she matched Mohanlal's eminence. Then, there was Innathe Chinthavishayam and Kamal's Minnaminnikkoottam.

Meera Jasmine still leads directors' wishlists for strong characters.
