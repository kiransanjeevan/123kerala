Boban to stamp his authority in a Postman's role

When Boban Kunchacko arrived on the scene, his impish presence, ready smile, attractive vibrancy and easy acting style all ensured that he was considered as an artiste with a lot of potential.

Underscoring this prognosis was the huge success of his films like Niram. And just when things were going well for him, the script took an unexpected turn. Suddenly, from being an actor with a bright future he was reduced to a struggling survivor.

To his eternal credit, it was not as if Boban was not trying. If anything, he could have been accused of over trying. He tried an image change. He did some serious roles. He worked on his roles. But none seemed to work.

It is perhaps just then that Boban must have told himself, why am I putting myself under so much pressure. I must start enjoying myself, rather than worry over things that I don't control.

Just as well. And it is showing during the shooting of Mayamohithachandran, his new movie after a considerable gap. He looks very relaxed and at ease, say unit hands.

The film's story itself is very relaxing. It's about a young and affable postman whom an entire village is fond of. A responsible person that he keeps postponing his marriage as he wants his sister to get married first. But things change with the arrival of a young girl from America.

"It is a tale of love, comedy and sentiments," says debutante director Shibu. "I am sure this will be accepted by the audience."

Boban himself, it is said, is pretty smugly satisfied with the film. And during the shooting schedule at Vadakkanchery, he comes across as calm and composed. If anything, the entire cast cuts a picture of one happy family. Even debutante heroine Renuka, a school student from Thrissur, looks the part.

The seniors like Oduvil Unnikrishnan, Nedumudi Venu, Bharath Gopi, Cochin Haneefa, Manoj K Jayan, Prithviraj, K P A C Lalitha, Ramya, Shaari, Geedha Salaam and Venu Machad are also very much involved in the whole project.

The other attraction of the film is the music by Ilayaraja.

On the whole, the film has everything to make it a commercial hit.
