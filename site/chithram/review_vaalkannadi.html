Review: Vaalkannadi

'Vaalkannaadi', which is the latest venture by director duo Anil-Babu after 'Pakalpooram', has got a good story line that seems to get slightly out of control somewhere during the course of narration. Kalabhavan Mani has given an excellent performance as Appu while Geethu Mohandas shines equally well in the role of Devu. Anil Murali is well cast as Thampan. Thilakan makes a comeback after a gap as the bedridden Raghavan. However, it is KPAC Lalitha, who, as Appu's mother Kuttiyamma, steals the show along with Kalabhavan Mani. Camerawork by Shaji, who had made his debut in the Anil-Babu-directed 'Uttaman', is very good. T.A.Razack has done good work of the script which is based on a story by him, but somehow it seems as if things get out of hand for the director and the scenarist after the interval, thus making the audience feel bored. The main lapse seems to be from the directorial department, for there are some emotional moments in the story that could have been a bit underplayed, making it more palatable. A more sensitive approach from the director's side could have made the film better.

Storyline
Appu (Appunni) is a young blacksmith belonging to Melur village. He has frequent bouts of madness, during which, he sometimes gets violent and harms others. During one such fit, he had attacked Raghavan, whom he had loved as a guru and as a father figure. Consequently Raghavan is crippled and becomes bedridden . Appunni is in love with Raghavan's young daughter Devu, who too is ready to sacrifice her life for Appu. Raghavan has forgiven Appu for unwittingly injuring him, but Devu's mother is not ready to forget things that easily. She wants Devu to marry someone else and not the 'mad' Appu. In the meantime Thampan, who had left the village years ago and who has been a childhood friend of Appu, re-appears. He gradually falls in love with Devu. Appu too feels that it is better that Devu marries Thampan and not him, for obvious reasons. He even talks to Thampan about it. But Devu convinces Appu not to forsake her. When Thampan realizes that things are getting out of control, he gets infuriated and swears to seek revenge. This leads to things getting worse and Appu again losing control of his mind. And during this bout of madness, something totally unexpected and unfortunate happens.
