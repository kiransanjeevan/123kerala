Preview: Mayavi

 
Mammootty plays Mahi alias Mayavi, a man who fights injustice in Shafi's Mayavi. The shooting of the film is fast progressing in Kochi under the direction of Shafi. Sanjeev Shankar wields camera for the film. An old building in the FACT is turned into a jail and Mammootty is inmate 555. His fellow inmates are Manoj K Jayan, Mamukoya, Keerikkadan Jose, Narayanankutty and Sagar Shiyas.

Mayavi is not a criminal but cannot stand injustice. He often takes the fall for his friends, and is a familiar face in prison. The film is a full-fledged entertainer with a couple of serious moments.

Gopika plays Indu, an educated villager, while Manoj K Jayan plays a bank employee who is found guilty of fraud.

The scenes being shot during the post-lunch session are in a hospital ward. Bad guys Vijayaraghavan, Spadikam George, P Sreekumar and Santosh Jogi are seen in bandages as they lie on adjacent beds. They complain about having been beaten up by an unknown person.

The film boasts a huge star cast that includes Sai Kumar, Cochin Haneefa, Salim Kumar and KPAC Lalitha.

The writer-director duo Rafi and Mecartin have scripted the movie which is being produced under the banner of Vaisakha films by P Rajan. Lyrics by Vayalar Sarat Chandra Varma have been set to tune by Alex Paul. The film is expected to hit the theatres in February.
