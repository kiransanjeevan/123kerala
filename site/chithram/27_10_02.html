Sobhana to get married

 

Finally, the beautiful Bharatnatyam danseur Sobhana has decided to get married. The groom of her choice is Rahul Mehra. For a very long time, Sobhana went about saying that she just had no time to fall in love and get married. But it looks as if she has managed to.

 
 
advertisement




 
 
Rahul Mehra is from Jharkhand. He also happens to be an agriculturist. The two met each other on the internet. What began as friendship slowly blossomed into love and is now ripening into marriage. The actress hasn't announced a marriage date yet, but you can rest assured that the day wouldn't be far.
