Titbits

 

Navya with Madhavan and Srikanth: Navya has bagged two Tamil films with Madhavan and Srikanth. In Ettappan, Navya will enact a Brahmin girl apposite Sreekanth, who is a rowdy. The shooting of the film will begin on October 20th and will be shot in Chennai and Ootty. After completing Ettappan, she will act with Madhavan in Vallamai Tharayo. Debutant Madhumita is directing the film. Madhupal's Thalappavu is Navya's Malayalam film.

Shamna with Mohanlal in two films: Upcoming actress Shamna, who acted as Mohanlal's sister in Ali Bhai, has bagged two more Mohanlal films. She will be seen in Thulasidas' College Kumaran and Sibi Malayil-S Bhasurachandran team's Flash. In College Kumaran, she plays Sreekutty, daughter of Nedumudi Venu and in Flash, she plays friend of Parvathy.

Shaan enters Kollywood: Following the success paths of Malayalam music directors like Jassie Gift and Vidyasagar in South Indian languages, another music director in Malayalam forms the lot, Shaan is entering Kollywood. He will be giving music to songs in the film Kai directed by Vinod Ragahav. The film which features Venket Bose and Charan Raj in the lead will have five songs set to music by Shaan. Shaan became famous in Mollywood with the music that he had given to Shaji Kailas thrillers like Tiger and Chinthamani Kola Case.
