Titbits

 

Manju Warrier is coming back?: She is known as one of the most gorgeous women the Malayalam industry has ever seen and today she happens to be the wife of one of the most senior heroes. We are talking about the drop dead beautiful Manju Warrier, who was seen in movies like 'Sallapam', 'Kanmadam' 'Kaliveedu' and many others. Now, there is news that this pretty woman might just be looking at making a comeback and the folks have been waiting eagerly for her to come up with an announcement.

Priyamani gets busy with big hero: The dusky buxom beauty Priyamani, who has become quite busy after winning the National Award is currently busy with the shooting of a new film that is being made in Telugu. Priya is pairing up with none other than the top hero Balakrishna and the shooting is on at the famous Ramoji Film city. This gives her a unique label since she has already acting with another hero from the same family. Good going Priyamani!!!

 
 





 
 
Mamatha to take the litmus test in Tamil: The slender Malayalam beauty Mamata Mohandas who has made her presence felt strongly in the Telugu industry is all set to repeat the act in Tamil now. Her new film 'Guru En Aalu' is all set to hit the screens this Friday and incidentally she is pairing up with the hot heartthrob Madhavan in this movie. This happens to be a remake of the Bollywood hit 'Yes Boss' that came in many years ago and had Shahrukh Khan as the hero. Mamata has been looking for a good break in Tamil so this might just be the one. All the best Mamata!!!

Jayaram is all smiles: Hero Jayaram who has been craving for a good break finally got his due with his Onam release 'Veruthe Oru Bharya' completing 50 days and also standing next to the record breaking collections of the super hit film 'Annan Thampi'. The film is still on at about twelve centres throughout the state and incidentally this was the last release of the dusky damsel Gopika before she got married and bid adieu to films.
