Shah Rukh at my feet, says Aamir

 

AAMIR KHAN is at it again. After taking potshots at Big B and ‘Black’ last year, it’s SRK who is Aamir’s latest punching bag. A few months ago, when Aamir was asked what would he like to ask Shah Rukh Khan, he replied tongue-in-cheek: “I would like to ask Shah Rukh how it feels to be number two.” While SRK did take it in the right spirit, there sure seems to some love lost between the two superstars. Now the latest comes in the form of Aamir’s blog where he talks about Shah Rukh licking his feet and asking for his attention.

And before you think that Aamir has lost it, the actor quickly clarifies that he is referring to his dog named Shah Rukh in his Panchgani home! In Aamir’s own words, "Shah Rukh is licking my feet and I am feeding him biscuits every now and then. What more can I ask for? Now, before you jump to any conclusions, let me add that Shah Rukh is the name of our dog. And before you jump to any further conclusions, let me add that I had nothing to do with naming him. In fact, Shah Rukh is the dog of the caretakers of our house. When I bought this house, it came with the caretakers and their dog!

 
 





 
 

"Apparently, Shah Rukh (the actor) was shooting for a commercial in this house a few years ago and that very day the caretakers bought a pup and named him Shah Rukh. Some of my friends claim that I bought the house just because of the dog. I would have to be a serious fan of Shah Rukh’s to want to do that!"

Even before signing off from his blog, he takes one final dig at the Baadshah of Bollywood by saying, “Well, Shah Rukh is once again begging for my attention; so let me get back to him. He is smelling too much. I think he needs a bath.”

Now we can’t wait to read what King Khan would have to say on Aamir talking about his dog Shah Rukh in his blog!
