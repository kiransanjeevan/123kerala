Preview: Meerayude Dukham Muthuvinte Swapnam

Director Vinayan is famous for rehashing the tearjerkers of yesteryears. He has been successful in serving this ‘kichidi’ to the audience of Kerala and his latest film Meerayude Dukham Muthuvinte Swapnam also falls in the same genre. This director feels that such films with characters having physical or mental deformities will work with the audience of Kerala.

So Vinayan is back to his favourite location in Kanjhar near Thodupuzha. He is trying to rehash his earlier mega hit Vasanthiyum Lakshmiyum Pinne Njanum in this sentimental sob story, which is sure to make his lady audience cry. Ambili Devi, the popular Television actress plays the title role of the physically handicapped Meera, who can hardly walk. The film focuses on her trials and tribulations. Her brother Muthu played by Pritviraj is the only person, kind and caring towards her. Renuka Menon, the heroine of Nammal plays Maya, daughter of Divakaran (Cochin Haneefa). She loves Muthu and helps him overcome all his worries.

The story revolves around the brother-sister love. Jagathy Sreekumar plays the role of Susheelan, Muthu’s uncle and the character has negative shades. He is an alcoholic who will do anything to get money and he even focuses on Meera to make some money. The cast also has Sudhish, Philomina, Yamuna, and Manga Mahesh. Music is by Mohan Sitara and camera, by Azhagappan. Editing is by G.Murali and Tom George produces the film.
