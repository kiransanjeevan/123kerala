Mamta admits to her B-town plans

 

Southern siren Mamta Mohandass is reported to be the latest export to Bollywood. The headturner doesn’t deny her B-town dreams completely, but confesses that she hasn’t yet “signed a Bollywood film.” Well, she’s been offered scripts and there have been discussions but nothing concrete has come of it so far. “When I was in Mumbai, I did take a closer look at some scripts but haven’t made any commitments.” However, like her counterparts Asin, Shriya Saran, Genelia D’Souza and Trisha, Mamta intends to expand her horizons and would like to do a Hindi film “if an exciting offer” comes her way.

Meanwhile, she is tied up with four Malyalam films with stars like Dileep and Prithvi. And she sounds all excited about playing a freedom fighter in an untitled period film. “I play an author-backed role in the film. It revolves round an orthodox Thamburatti girl who is depressed about being married to an old man. Then she joins the Khilafat Movement and overcomes stiff resistance from a male-chauvinistic society. I'll do some homework to get my look and gait right,”she says.

 
 





 
 
Her last Tollywood outing was King, after which Mamta hasn’t been visible here. She says that she’s got “plenty of offers” but wants “to come back with a meaty role.” Similarly, she hasn’t done any Tamil film after Guru Namma Allu since “nothing concrete happened” till date.

Until Mumbai ensnares her, this multi-lingual star seems to be having the best of the southern film industry. And her fans sure aren’t complaining.
