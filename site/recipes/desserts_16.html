Filled Pan Cake
200g maida flour
1 egg
50 gram sugar
1 cup coconut milk
1/2 cup water

Mix all the ingredients together , if it is too thick add a bit more water, or add a bit more flour according to its consistency. bake the pan cakes and keep aside.
