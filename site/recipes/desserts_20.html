Simple Banana Cake
Ingredients

Bananas-4
cardamom-3
sugar- to taste
oil-for frying
wheat flour-enough to make a loose dough

advertisement

Preparation Method

Mash the bananas,sugar and cardamom in mixie well.To it add wheat flour so that the dough is little loose than chapathi dough.wet the hand with water and make small balls and fry in oil till it becomes golden brown colour.
