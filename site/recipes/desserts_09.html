Burfi

Ingredients
Badam:250gms.
almonds,250gms
sugar,1/2tsp
2 silverleaves.

advertisement

Method of Preparation

Soak almonds in warm water overnight.peel and grind in a mixer to a fine uniform paste. Now boil sugar in a cup of water and prepare thick syrup. add almonds(paste)and stir well.cook for 5 minutes on slow fire.add cardamoms and remove from fire. grease a plate with ghee and spread the paste evenly. decorate with silver leaves and set aside,till cool. cut into square or diamond shaped pieces as you desire and serve..
