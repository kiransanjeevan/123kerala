Kerala's tipplers are turning to brandy from rum

KOCHI: Are Kerala's tipplers turning from rum to brandy? Market reports seem to indicate it. The success of market leader UB Group, with a wide and varied portfolio, in marketing its No 1 McDowell brandy in a new bottle strongly endorses the trend of favouring brandy.

According to Alok Gupta, Group Divisional vice-president (Marketing), the market share of the brand has grown from 18 per cent to 24 per cent in over two months. This will be sustained, he feels, as the Kerala market is growing at 20 per cent.

Of the total liquor market of 10 million cases in the State, rum is still strong with 58 per cent, brandy 36 per cent, and whisky just 2.5 per cent share. But according to Gupta, the regular brandy market will be 15 per cent and the regular rum market slightly higher at 16 per cent. But the difference comes in cheap stuff.

About 50 per cent of the rum market is dominated by cheaper stuff. With a record sales of 3 lakh cases, the No.1 McDowell brand is the 5th largest brandy in the world in sales. Its 750-ml bottle has been re-packaged with a new design hard to replicate, he said. The brand is produced from UB's distilleries in Thrissur and Cherthala and, hence, it has a large manufacuring presence in the state.

Almost 95 per cent of total brandy sales in the country is in the South. Besides theb State, Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh are also large brandy markets.
