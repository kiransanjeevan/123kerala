BharatMatrimony.com launches ‘Veriprofile’


First of its kind ‘prospective life partner and self verification’ service introduced in the Indian online industry

Chennai, October 27th, 2004…BharatMatrimony.com the No 1 Indian Matrimony Portal, today launched a first of its kind initiative, the ‘VeriProfile’ service. VeriProfile will help millions of registered singles only at BharatMatrimony.com to verify the details of prospective life partners with out revealing their own identity. Also, a person can have his/her own profile certified. VeriProfile couples a unique offline and online model, which will be available across all major and mini metros in India. VeriProfile is pending patent.

Speaking at the launch of the service Murugavel Janakiraman, CEO BharatMatrimony.com said, “The internet has evolved extensively and successfully to provide a whole new dimension to match making, however, seekers of life partners have often questioned this medium for the genuineness of the people they meet online. As the No.1 player in the matrimonial services space BharatMatrimony.com has a moral responsibility towards making Internet matrimony totally reliable and wiser choice for the Indian community.”

Prospective Partner Verification

A BharatMatrimony.com member can initiate profile verification of a prospective life partner. This Prospective life partner receives a request from Bharatmatrimony.com, if the request is accepted, then the profile information is processed for verification. The verification process will be completed within 7 working days and the information will be validated in person by the verification agency and also against bonafide documents. The verification certificate will be given to both parties. However, if the request is declined, the process is terminated.

Self Verification

Any member of BharatMatrimony.com can also initiate verification on himself / herself to enhance the credibility for his /her profile and thereby increase the number of matrimonial responses. The verification certificate will be given to the member.

How does Veriprofile work?

Details that are verified:

VeriProfile seeks to verify the following details Name, Age, Income, Marital Status, Address, Family Background and Blood Group.

There are two distinct process of VERIPROFILE.

Prospective Partner Verification

* A BharatMatrimony.com member, can verify the details of a prospective life partner, who is also a member of Bharatmatrimony.com, by clicking on the VeriProfile link available on his/her profile.

http://www.bharatmatrimony.com/verify.shtml
* A request will be sent via an email to the prospective life partner, letting him/her know that, there has been a request from a member for the verification of his/her profile.
* The identity of the requesting party will not be revealed.
* The prospective life partner will then have to either ACCEPT or DECLINE the request.
* If he/she ACCEPTS the request, they then have to submit their details in an online form.
* These details are then sent to the Verification Agency. Verification would be completed within 7 working days.
* The verification Agency, by making prior appointments, send their agents to verify the details of the member.
* Once verified, a report is made and sent to the requesting party as well as the person verified as an email and the same report is sent to the party verified. So he/she may use it for further requests.

HOWEVER if the prospective life partner, does not want to submit to the verification and DECLINES the request, A mail is sent to the requesting party , intimating them and the process is terminated .

Self Verification

* Any member of BharatMatrimony.com can also initiate verification on himself/herself by clicking on the VeriProfile link available on his/her profile.
* He/ She then has to submit their details in an online form.
* These details are then sent to the Verification Agency.
* The verification Agency, by making prior appointments, send their agents to verify the details of the member.
* Once verified , a report is made available to the them on a url . So he/she may use it for further requests. Verification would be completed within 7 days .
