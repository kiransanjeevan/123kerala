Kandahar VS Christian brothers

 

Mohanlal who has found good hits in the recent “Shikaar” will have to sort out a new problem in the coming weeks. As his two movies, both big budgeters are getting ready to grace the theatres next month. Lal will have to talk to the producers to delay one of them for atleast two weeks so that the runnning of one of them won't effect the other.

 

 



The two movies on discussion are Major Ravi's Kandahar and Joshy's Christian Brothers. Both the movies have comleted their shoting and is almost ready for release. Subair the producer of Christian Brothers is said to have already committed the release to 100 theatres.

.

 

 

As per the orginal schedule, Christian Brothers was poised to greet the theatres on October 28. But the little delay in shooting has made the producers to reshedule the release dates to November third week. Kandahar too was orginally planned for November, but will have to look for a date in December to avoid clashes. Any how Superstar Mohanlal who wil return after his Bangkok shoot of Casanova will say the final word on the release of his two films. And as per helivable souces, Mohanlal is expected to suggest Christian Brothers release on November 20 while Kandahar may get to theatres on December 9 or 16

.
